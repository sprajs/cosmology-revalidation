# Claim-and-evidence ledger

Opened 2026-09-20. This is an evidence ledger, not a count of agreeing investigators. Repeated analyses of the same SNe do not become independent observations. Numerical products are linked to their branch reports and manifests. An entry labelled conditional remains conditional even if numerical reproduction succeeds.
