# Physical foundations

These chapters derive the equations connecting supernova emission, propagation, measurement and cosmological inference. They distinguish physical identities from empirical parameterizations and assumptions that still require observational tests.

| Chapter | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Luminosity](luminosity.md) | Radioactive decay, deposition, expansion, diffusion, emergent spectra and the limits of trained spectral models |
| [Extinction](extinction.md) | Grain cross sections, radiative transfer, attenuation laws, broad-band response and dust geometry |
| [Observation chain](observation-chain.md) | Redshift, photon integration, detector response, calibration and fitted supernova coordinates |
| [Cosmology and populations](cosmology-populations.md) | Expansion equations, distances, cosmic time, population delays and probe assumptions |
| [Equation index](equation-index.json) | Stable equation labels and current chapter locations |

The derivations are retained from the September 21 equation audit. Links to implementation-specific evidence point to the preserved source snapshot, since those historical checks are not all part of the current executable workflow set. The [historical findings](findings.json) retain their original source identities and dates. They do not represent new fits made during the repository reorganization.

For current calculations and their numerical checks, use the [workflow guide](../workflows.md), [methods](../methods/README.md) and [validation guide](../../validation/README.md). The chapters provide physical context; they do not certify every imported survey or CMB implementation.
