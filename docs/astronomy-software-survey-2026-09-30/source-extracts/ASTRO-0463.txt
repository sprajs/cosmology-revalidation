dimensional data arrays.

A Description of the CUPID applications

 CLUMPINFO – Obtain information about one or more previously
identified clumps 
 CUPIDHELP – Display information about CUPID

 EXTRACTCLUMPS – Extract previously identified clumps of emission
from an NDF 
 FINDBACK – Estimate the background in an NDF by
removing small scale structure 
 FINDCLUMPS – Identify clumps of
emission within a 1, 2 or 3 dimensional NDF 
 MAKECLUMPS – Create
simulated data containing clumps and noise 
 OUTLINECLUMP –
Draw an outline around a 2-dimensional clump identified by CUPID
