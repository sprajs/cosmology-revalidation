# RAISIN photometry flux-sign integrity audit

The 117 physical files in the pinned RAISIN photometry release contain 23,007 reported `FLUXCAL` observations: **23,007 positive, zero negative, zero zero**, with finite positive quoted errors. This is a property of the released photometry, not an estimate of the underlying noise law. The 17 coordinate-linked DES optical light curves show a stronger lineage result: the author’s earlier DES16 force-photometry files include 2,243 negative measurements that are absent from the RAISIN products, while all 3,135 positive measurements appear at the same band and time. The transformation code that made those products has not been identified, so the observed sign-dependent retention is established, but a specific source-code mechanism is not.

The inventory and joins were specified in [the frozen protocol](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/protocol.json) before inspecting the sign outcomes, with the historical phase-window [amendment](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/phase-window-amendment.json) frozen before retention outcomes. The optical join key was the DES coordinate alias, band, and `|ΔMJD| ≤ 0.00055` day; a wider `0.01` day comparison was diagnostic only. Candidate rows and duplicates are retained rather than resolved by flux. No arbitrary flux scale was fitted. The original DES `FLUXCAL` convention is `mag = 27.5 − 2.5 log10(FLUXCAL)` in [the DIFFIMG README](../../data/des-diffimg/DES-SN5YR_DIFFIMG.README); the author files carry the same `FLUXCAL`/`FLUXCALERR` columns. Their separate `ZPFLUX` column describes the exposure zero point, not another unit for these numbers.

## Released DES joins and relevant phase

All 17 released RAISIN DES photometry files have Git blob hashes **byte-identical** to the corresponding files in the pinned author repository tree; see [author-product-blob-ledger.csv](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/author-product-blob-ledger.csv). Thus these release rows were not revised after that author product. Of 5,112 RAISIN optical rows for these aliases, 5,111 find a unique original DES-SN5YR DIFFIMG row by the primary band/time key. Their original fluxes are positive. The original-to-product ledger preserves two time-duplicate ambiguities, so 5,108 distinct original rows are marked retained. For the 5,108 distinct retained original rows, the median absolute flux difference is about `0.0001` FLUXCAL units, and 5,107 are within an absolute `0.001` (one float/rounding boundary). The quoted errors do **not** have the same exact precision: median absolute difference is `0.0018`, maximum `2.051`. Only 1,129 RAISIN rows meet a joint `0.001` flux-and-error bound. The alternative DES SMP release supplies a primary-time match for 1,289/5,112 rows, 133 of whose original fluxes are negative, but none has a joint decimal flux/error match; it is a different photometric reduction, not the exact RAISIN optical ancestor.

In the original DIFFIMG series of these 17 aliases, the [row ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/original-retention.csv) covers 9,843 optical rows: **0/4,229 negative** rows and **5,108/5,614 positive** rows are present in RAISIN. The 506 absent positives are all outside the historical optical fitting rest-phase window; their `PHOTFLAG` values and raw FITS row pointers are retained in that ledger. Within the historical `TREST_REJECT=-7,45` optical window specified by the pinned `lcfitting/REFAC_DES_RAISIN_optical_sys.nml`, **0/56 negative and 811/811 positive** original rows are retained. Within the separately stated M20 `[-10,40]` descriptive window, the corresponding counts are 0/47 and 836/836. The NML has empty `EPCUT_SNRMIN`; no positive-only cut is specified there. This supports a sign-selection concern in the photometry upstream of that fitting configuration, but does not establish which epochs passed every later fit/model/filter rule.

The single RAISIN optical row without a primary DIFFIMG match is `DES16S2afz` z/MJD 58030.383, `+61.55±154.197`. A 0.0028-day secondary-time candidate is `−61.557±208.670` (DIFFIMG FITS row 10,374,797, `PHOTFLAG=2049`). Its error and time differ, so it is an unresolved reduction/revision exception, **not** proof of sign folding. The two retained-row duplicate cases are original DIFFIMG row 9,360,381 (`DES15C3odz`, three RAISIN same-time candidates) and 11,688,982 (`DES16C3cmy`, two); the [candidate ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/match-candidates.csv) preserves all alternatives.

## Earlier author force-photometry precursor

The author tree’s 17 original DES files and README were acquired by exact Git blob identity and pinned in [author-acquisition.json](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/author-acquisition.json). The README identifies the source as DiffImg `makeDataFiles_fromSNforce` version 2.3 and defines `PHOTFLAG` bit meanings. The [author-lineage.csv](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/author-lineage.csv) ledger preserves each raw file, source line, observation index, band, MJD, signed flux/error, flag, product candidate count, and later DIFFIMG FITS-row candidate.

The ten DES16 precursor files contain 5,378 optical rows: 3,135 positive and 2,243 negative. Every positive row occurs in the released product at the same band/time; no negative row does. Among the 3,132 unique positive matches, product flux differs from source by at most `0.0005` FLUXCAL units, consistent with three-decimal product rounding. However, product versus precursor error differs by a median `0.0017`, 90th percentile `0.004`, and maximum `0.134`; later DIFFIMG errors sometimes differ more. Of 2,243 source-negative rows, 2,238 have a unique same-band/time later DIFFIMG row, 2,237 of those remain negative, and one (`DES16C1cim`) has a revised positive later flux but no RAISIN product row. The near-identical flux lineage therefore does **not** establish a byte-exact error transformation or a complete unchanged measurement likelihood. The signed-augmentation [handoff](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/lineage-handoff.json) and [negative-candidate ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_flux_sign_audit/author-negative-candidates.csv) preserve all 2,243 DES16 source-negative rows, of which 33 lie in header-peak `[-7,45]` rest-phase; native fitting must make its own phase/filter decision. No sign, SNR, or `PHOTFLAG` cut was imposed on the candidate ledger.

The seven DES15 author precursors are much shorter, earlier in-season reductions (368 optical rows, 43 negative). Their positive fluxes differ materially from the later RAISIN/DIFFIMG products (median absolute difference `2.5` among 274 unique positive-time matches). Eight raw-negative times collide with positive product values, but fluxes and errors changed; that is revision/remeasurement ambiguity rather than evidence of absolute-value folding. They cannot be pooled with the DES16 near-identical-flux lineage as an exact restoration.

The pinned author `update_phot.py` only rewrites HST J/H values from positive-magnitude photometry and copies the optical rows; `add_csp_calibs.py` addresses three CSP calibrators. Neither explains this DES optical sign retention. The missing link is the exact earlier optical product-generation script and error convention, plus execution evidence of the historical fit and bias-correction path. A paired SNooPy signed-row refit could therefore test a **conditional** photometry-augmentation sensitivity, not reconstruct a proven historical counterfactual or infer a distance/cosmology correction by itself.
