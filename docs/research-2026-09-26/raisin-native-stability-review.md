# Independent native RAISIN signed-refit stability review

The 240 modern native SNooPy fits reproduce the reported multistart instability. I reparsed all 24 FITRES/LCPLOT/log outputs (three starts × free/fixed peak × four photometry arms, ten objects each) and rebuilt each three-start comparison. All 240 `ERRFLAG_FIT` values are zero and all fit-parameter covariance matrices exported on `PHASE2_HESSIAN` lines have a positive minimum eigenvalue for the relevant three fixed-peak or four free-peak dimensions (smallest `2.58×10⁻⁸`). `RV=1.518` with zero reported error in every fit; fixed runs have `PKMJD=PKMJDINI` and zero peak error, while free runs report a positive peak error. These checks establish native completion and internal covariance signs, **not** global numerical convergence or a physical distance correction.

Among 80 object/timing/arm comparisons, 28 have a three-start `DLMAG` range greater than `0.001` mag, and 20 exceed `0.01` mag. The largest is `DES16C1cim`, free-peak hybrid arm H: `DLMAG` range `0.2240638733` mag, AV range `0.1326821558`, stretch range `0.1938450336`, and peak-date range `1.52734375` days. Its native accepted epoch mask is identical across starts. The largest improvement from the initial start in exported **data `FITCHI2`** among same-mask cases is `1.154876709` for that fit. This is not a verified change in the **full optimizer objective**: [the executed SNANA source](../../phase2/official/build/SNANA-current/src/snlc_fit.F90) states that `CHI2INI` prior terms enter minimization but are excluded from stored `FITCHI2` (around lines 3941–45, 4131–39 and 4453). The NML retains a five-day peak-MJD prior, and `FITINI_PRIORS` initializes it even if other priors are disabled. Without independently reconstructing all native prior terms, lower exported `FITCHI2` is a data-fit comparison, not a basis to choose a preferred global minimum.

The accepted masks are equal across starts for 76/80 cases. The four differences are **all four free-peak arms of `DES16C3cmy`**. Start `0.85` accepts three fewer optical epochs than starts `1.0` and `1.15`: the same g, r and z exposures near MJD 57686.3 (exact printed source rows in [result.json](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/result.json)). Thus even their exported data `FITCHI2` values are not comparable at a fixed observation set. The [independently reconstructed comparison ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/comparison-checks.csv) and every same-start B−A/H−R distance shift reproduce the frozen multistart and paired-sensitivity CSVs, without selecting a start.

Input and native-export lineage also closes. The 114 frozen primary input hashes and all 36 stability NML hashes agree with current files, as do the 24 execution/log/protocol links. The ten R files are byte-identical to released RAISIN products, as are the zero-change R copies; per-object A has exactly the 3,135 author-positive optical rows, B has all 5,378 signed author optical rows, and H preserves the R prefix and appends all 2,243 author-negative rows. The [input-lineage ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/input-lineage.csv) verifies row multiplicity, not just totals. In every native run, all optical input rows appear one-to-one in nonmodel LCPLOT rows with matching band/date/flux/error/sign at native printed precision. R/A each export 3,135 positive optical rows; B/H each export 5,378, including all 2,243 negatives. In the initial free fit, 33 signed negative rows are accepted in B and 33 in H; R/A accept none. The maximum native-versus-input printed date deviation is `0.0019` day. No flag cut was inserted by this review.

The [review plan](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/review-plan.json) was saved after the root result was known, so this is an independent verification rather than blinded validation. [Machine results](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/result.json), [per-fit checks](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/fit-checks.csv) and the [manifest](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_native_stability_review/manifest.json) preserve hashes and exact comparisons. No native fit was rerun or original result edited. The practical conclusion remains conditional: start dependence and changing peak-dependent masks prevent treating one signed-minus-positive fitted distance as a closed historical correction or cosmology result.
