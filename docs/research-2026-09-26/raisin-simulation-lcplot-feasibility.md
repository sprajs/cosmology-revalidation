# Archived DES NIR simulation LCPLOT input feasibility

The nominal author blob `output/fit_nir/DES_RAISIN_NIR_SIM/DES_RAISIN_SIM/FITOPT000.LCPLOT.gz` at RAISIN_cosmo commit `b888214a5cbae38ac0bf886488ce7734f5b77e87` was acquired alone, without simulation photometry. Its 972,585 compressed bytes match Git blob `d81d64d3f1f395ad9dbbab71837d89f4811320af`; SHA-256 is `6aeb02bab3648daad6435405bbd1c9ab7f8f4db6949bf24ab00cba7296de6f97`. See the [acquisition record](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_execution_review/nominal_fitres/lcplot-feasibility/acquisition.json) and [metadata inventory](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_execution_review/nominal_fitres/lcplot-feasibility/inventory.json). No flux residual, distance, or timing-difference analysis was performed for this feasibility check.

Its sole `VARNAMES` line is `CID MJD Tobs SIM_FLUXCAL FLUXCAL FLUXCAL_ERR DATAFLAG BAND CHI2 IFIT BAND_REST FLUX_REST KCOR AVWARP SIM_FLUX_REST`. There are 71,029 `OBS:` lines and no malformed rows: 69,200 `DATAFLAG=0` plotted fit/model points and 1,829 `DATAFLAG=1` measured data rows; no `DATAFLAG=-1` rejected-data rows. The flag meaning is explicit in pinned v11_04k [`sntools_output_text.c`](../../sources/repos/RickKessler__SNANA@v11_04k/src/sntools_output_text.c:893). Among data rows, 454 are J and 1,375 H; each of the 500 plotted CIDs has 2–6 measured rows. All 71,029 rows have `IFIT=1`, and `(CID, printed MJD, BAND)` has no duplicate. `DATAFLAG=0` rows are plotted model samples, not additional observations.

The 500 plotted IDs are exactly CID 1–500 and match the *first 500 FITRES rows in order*. No plotted rows exist for the remaining 29,500 nominal NIR FITRES CIDs. The nominal NML sets `MXLC_PLOT=500`, and pinned [`snana.car`](../../sources/repos/RickKessler__SNANA@v11_04k/src/snana.car:22455) applies that cap by plot count. This is a processing-order cap, not a timing or fit-outcome selection in this inventory. The source plot packer reads MJD, measured flux, total flux error, and simulated epoch flux from stored SNLC arrays and computes `Tobs = MJD − SNLC_SEARCH_PEAKMJD` ([`snana.car`](../../sources/repos/RickKessler__SNANA@v11_04k/src/snana.car:22679)). The text writer prints MJD to 0.001 day, Tobs to 0.01 day, and measured flux, error, and `SIM_FLUXCAL` with four decimals in scientific mantissas (five significant digits) ([`sntools_output_text.c`](../../sources/repos/RickKessler__SNANA@v11_04k/src/sntools_output_text.c:1558)). These printed values cannot reproduce the binary fitter inputs bit for bit.

For CID 1–500, the `DATAFLAG=1` lines offer a **partial, rounded** NIR observation table: band, epoch, measured flux/error and simulated true flux. Matching FITRES has redshift, MWEBV, `SIM_LIBID`, fitted parameters and four-decimal `PKMJDINI`; the NML names the KCOR and SNooPy assets. But LCPLOT omits original full-precision photometry, per-epoch PHOTFLAG and other raw quality metadata, the complete original simulation HEAD/PHOT records, and all data for the other 29,500 CIDs. A `DATAFLAG=1` line can be treated as plotted data, with source flag semantics, but cannot independently prove every upstream photometry/filter/covariance choice. `Tobs` is derived from the search peak and is rounded; it is not an independent timing measurement.

The two nominal FITRES files share 29,987 CIDs and their printed simulation truth fields, while their NMLs both name `DES_RAISIN_SIM` as `VERSION_PHOTOMETRY`. This supports a common nominal photometry version, but **does not establish epoch-by-epoch equality of the noise realizations or accepted masks between NIR and optical+NIR fits**: only the NIR LCPLOT was acquired in this bounded check, and the FITRES tables do not contain per-epoch fluxes. A paired intervention requiring identical epochs/noise needs original simulation HEAD/PHOT identity or an independently checked optical+NIR epoch table. For an approximate partial check, the first 500 NIR objects are available without any outcome-based CID choice; they cannot stand in for all 30,000 archived simulated attempts.
