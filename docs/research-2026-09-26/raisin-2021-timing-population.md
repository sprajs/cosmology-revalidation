# Coherent 2021 RAISIN simulation timing metadata

The original-author **2021-11-11** nominal DES NIR and optical+NIR FITRES snapshots were audited without a native fit or a distance outcome. Their exact Git blobs, acquisition paths and SHA-256 hashes are in the [source acquisition](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/raisin_timing_assets/historical-tables-acquisition.json). The [protocol](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/protocol.json) and [complete executable hash freeze](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/execution-freeze.json) preceded numeric summaries. The FITRES headers name photometry version `DES_RAISIN_SIM` and **SNANA `v11_04d`**. This is a coherent author-file *snapshot*; no generator executable, raw HEAD/PHOT, seed or log hash proves all upstream execution settings.

`SIM_PKMJD` is the printed simulated generating peak, `PKMJDINI` the fit initializer/header-derived peak, and `PKMJD` the fit output, all in observer MJD. The NIR table contains **30,000** unique CIDs; optical+NIR contains **29,995** unique CIDs. Their CID intersection is all 29,995 optical+NIR rows, with **zero printed mismatches** in `PKMJDINI`, `SIM_PKMJD`, `SIM_LIBID`, simulated stretch/AV/RV and the inspected redshifts. Five NIR CIDs have no joint row: **12988, 15434, 25042, 26060, 27456** ([metadata ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/missing-joint.csv)). All five NIR rows report `ERRFLAG_FIT=0` and `CUTFLAG_SNANA=3`; their *joint* absence has no per-CID cause in the two FITRES files or batch-level `SUBMIT.INFO`. We have not called them failed fits or selected them out. All retained rows in both tables have `ERRFLAG_FIT=0`, `CUTFLAG_SNANA=3` and `SIM_TYPE_INDEX=1`. No truth-Ia cut or distance variable was used to form a cohort.

For **all 30,000 NIR rows**, `PKMJD=PKMJDINI` exactly at print precision; `PKMJDINI−SIM_PKMJD` has mean −0.000391 day and sample SD **0.010119 day**. On the 29,995 common rows, 20,105 initializers are within ±0.01 day of `SIM_PKMJD`, 29,893 within ±0.03 day, and all within ±0.05 day. The joint fitted-minus-simulated peak median is −0.0078 day, 5th–95th percentile −0.7969 to +0.5781 days, and full printed range −6.9883 to +2.7422 days. These [quantiles and fixed z bins](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/result-v2.json) show the **near-truth NIR timing issue persists in the coherent 2021 pair**: a fixed NIR initializer has ~0.01-day scatter around simulation truth, whereas the optical+NIR fitted peak is much broader. This is a property of these simulated metadata, not a measured timing error in real RAISIN SNe. The independent standard-library [join/arithmetic verification](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/independent-verification.json) reproduces the main means, sample SDs and medians.

The entire matched sample has `zHEL` median **0.41227**, range **0.28785–0.61665**; the preset plotted 500 have median **0.41106**, range **0.29068–0.61442**. The first eight have median 0.38839, range 0.29359–0.47004. The fixed bins and all robust quantiles are preserved in the result rather than redefining a redshift window after inspecting timing. No substantive claim of sampling equivalence is made from those summaries.

The exact-commit [NIR NML](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/author/fit/sim/REFAC_DES_RAISIN_NIR.nml:63) sets shape=1, AV=0 and peak-step=0, fits `JH`, and sets RV initializer 1.518; all NIR FITRES rows have zero printed peak, stretch, AV and RV errors, with fixed values 1, 0 and 1.518 as applicable. The exact-commit [optical+NIR NML](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/author/fit/sim/REFAC_DES_RAISIN_optnir.nml:63) comments out those step-zero settings and fits `grizJH`. Its peak, stretch and AV errors are strictly positive for all 29,995 rows, while RV remains 1.518 with zero error ([field ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/nuisance-fields.json)). Both NMLs use a 5-day peak prior and the same SNooPy B18 model path; the [NIR](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/author/output/fit_nir/DES_RAISIN_NIR_SIM/SUBMIT.INFO) and [joint](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/author/output/fit_all/DES_RAISIN_OPTNIR_SIM/SUBMIT.INFO) submission records name these NMLs and nominal FITOPT000 jobs completed on 2021-11-11. That provenance supports configuration association but does not supply the expanded executed input or binary hash.

The 500 plotted CIDs have an independently verified same-CID [2021 FITRES/LCPLOT clock, accepted-row and cadence join](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/raisin_timing_assets/historical-coherence-result.json). A provenance issue was caught during this audit: the inherited `all500-membership.csv` gives **2022-era** z/LIBID fields for the same CID. Those differ from 2021 FITRES for 477/500 (`zHEL`) and 458/500 (`SIM_LIBID`), though its fixed CID list and J/H counts happen to agree. The original [v1 result](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/result.json) remains untouched; a [pre-plot-count amendment](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/plot-cadence-amendment.json) and [v2 result](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/result-v2.json) obtain accepted band counts directly from the 2021-coherent LCPLOT. All 500 match NIR `NDOF+1`, totaling **454 J and 1,375 H** accepted epochs. The fixed [first-eight ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_2021_timing_population/first8-v2.csv) now carries the 2021 plot counts. No 2022 z, LIBID or timing value entered the final 2021 summaries.

For comparison only, the separate [later 2022 audit](raisin-simulation-timing-audit.md) had 30,000 NIR but 29,987 joint rows, initializer SD 0.010103 day and joint peak-minus-truth SD 0.545335 day, with a later source/version snapshot. Its figures were **not pooled** with 2021; their similar scale does not establish identical event realization, noise or fit execution.
