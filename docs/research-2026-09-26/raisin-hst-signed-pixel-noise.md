# Current HST F160W signed-pixel repeat-noise check

This is a current-product extraction control for the public 2026-reprocessed WFC3/IR images near DES16E1dcx. It is not a replay of RAISIN's historical difference photometry, a CRNL correction, or a supernova-distance result. The frozen design and sources are in `runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_design/` and the executed ledgers in `pixel_execution/`.

The pre-outcome Stage A used exposure ordinals 1 and 3 in both the search and template visits to define source masks and design background. It identified 385 WCS-only lattice positions, excluded 175 by the fixed design source mask, and evaluated quality support on 210. The strict `DQ==0` operator had **zero** positions that passed all eight images, so its primary branch stopped at the prespecified minimum of 30. The separately frozen masked-pixel secondary retained 170 positions across all 16 spatial tiles, with 7–16 per tile. Its coverage requires at least 90% of PAM-weighted aperture area and 75% of annulus area in every exposure; no aperture-coverage rescaling was applied. Stage A took 78.74 s under its 120 s benchmark cap, and all operator and mask hashes closed.

Stage B was released separately for the masked secondary. It wrote the complete 1,360 exposure, 340 pair, and 170 common-template ledgers, then failed while serializing a NumPy integer tile ID to the primary JSON file. The failed log and frozen executor remain intact. An additive recovery used only those saved ledgers; it did not reopen FITS data or extract new apertures. An independent ledger parser recomputed the primary statistics, all 16 delete-one tile values, all 2×2,000 tile bootstrap replicates, and 170 common-template identities. These comparisons passed. The planned strict-only radius/reversal sensitivities have no eligible cohort and were not run.

Root's separate raw-image validator re-extracted all 1,360 exposure rows and reconstructed all 340 repeat pairs and 170 shared-template rows. It passed with maximum absolute flux difference `4.33×10⁻¹⁵` and quoted-variance difference `4.31×10⁻⁹`; the latter reflects float64 versus production float32 ERR-squared arithmetic. This checks extraction arithmetic and provenance, not the physical completeness of the ERR model. The validator's full ledger and hashes are in `pixel_execution/root-raw-pixel-review/`.

The count-weighted pooled mean z² is 0.72080. Centered variance ratios are 0.71062 (search) and 0.73758 (template); the fixed spatial-tile bootstrap intervals are [0.58233, 0.84278] and [0.52209, 1.02469]. These are descriptive resampling ranges, not physical-null confidence intervals or p-values. The raw moment Σ(d²−V) has zero expectation only under zero-mean selected contrasts and correctly quoted variance for the **selected operator**; Σd²/ΣV is a random-denominator ratio, not an unbiased pivot. No ERR rescaling follows from either number.

The [two-panel spatial view](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_execution/secondary-spatial-signed-z.png) plots the 170 same locations in search and template, with fixed signed-z color limits ±3 and marker area set by quoted pair variance. Its visual encoding was frozen in `pixel_execution/plot-protocol.json` before rendering. It is a descriptive view of the saved pair ledger, not another statistical test.

For the same 170 positions, the mean common-template cross-product minus separate-template cross-product is 0.07038 in the extraction's flux units squared. The diagonal quoted-variance algebra predicts a mean common-template off-diagonal covariance of 0.12379. These are distinct descriptive quantities; their difference cannot by itself isolate a detector covariance or a physical correction. The operator and test images can retain image flat-field, static-scene, masking, cross-pixel, and cross-exposure effects. The design split, current calibration and WCS/PAM operator also differ from the private historical RAISIN image subtraction. The result therefore provides a conditional current-image noise/scene diagnostic, not historical error-model validation or a CRNL finding.
