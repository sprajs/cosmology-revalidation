# Public HST image-identification feasibility for DES16E1dcx

The [RAISIN paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07801v2) gives DES16E1dcx at RA 00:35:52.440, Dec −43:21:29.232 (8.9685°, −43.35812°) and identifies RAISIN2 as HST GO-14216, PI Kirshner. Its photometry section says the authors started from MAST FLT images, drizzled each epoch, aligned it to a later template, subtracted the template and measured aperture photometry with fake-star host-noise checks. Those private intermediate products are not identified by a public association image merely because its date and filter match. The representative DES_RAISIN SIMLIB LIBID11 header points elsewhere on the sky; I used the **paper's actual SN coordinate** for the archive search.

Before querying MAST I froze the [30-arcsec cone and six source-epoch matching criteria](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/protocol.json). The official [MAST CAOM cone](https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/_services.html) returned 78 metadata rows (response [hash and size](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/caom-summary.json)); 30 were HST, and 20 HST rows were GO-14216 WFC3/IR at target `DES16E1DCX`. The archive pointing is 0.45 arcsec from the paper coordinate. All relevant rows show `PUBLIC` access and PI Robert P. Kirshner. The complete [observation ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/all-hst-caom.csv) preserves other cone hits, including unrelated MIRVIS/F645N calibrations, HLA and skycell products.

Each of the six published SIMLIB times falls within its matching archive group and 3.6–5.3 minutes after group start. Visit 4 provides same-target, same-filter later imaging consistent with the paper's template strategy. MAST also lists a second HST association-level representation for each filter/visit (`hst_14216_i*_wfc3_ir_*_icxoi*`), plus four combined `detection` entries; these overlap the same dates and must not be counted as independent visits. The [eight-row visit ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/visit-groups.csv) retains exact CAOM observation IDs, start/end times, rights and archive `dataURL` URIs. Each association row has CAOM calibration level 3 and a public `mast:HST/product/..._drz.fits` URI. [MAST's field definitions](https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/_c_a_o_mfields.html) distinguish this science-product level from lower-level calibrated exposures.

The public **program, target, filter and visit associations are identifiable without ambiguity at this level**. The initial metadata-only audit stopped when the official [CAOM Products](https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/_services.html) endpoint timed out on both bounded per-observation and combined-ID attempts ([acquisition log](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/acquisition-log.json)). It downloaded no FITS data and did not invent constituent names. The separate header follow-up below resolves part of that acquisition gap.

## Header-only follow-up: constituent exposures and calibration versions

Root used the documented [MAST Download API](https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/MastApiTutorial.html) with HTTP byte ranges. A [two-product protocol](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/protocol.json) selected the first F160W association and its later template before acquisition; a [separate extension](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/remaining_visits/protocol.json) selected all six remaining pre-identified associations. The client stopped at each primary-header END card. All requests returned HTTP206. It retained 529,920 header bytes and read no science pixels.

The headers identify **32 distinct constituent FLT exposures, four per association**. Their exposure-time sums agree with each association's EXPTIME. First-visit F160W inputs are `icxoi1b9q`, `icxoi1bcq`, `icxoi1bfq`, and `icxoi1biq`; template inputs are `icxoi4hdq`, `icxoi4hgq`, `icxoi4hjq`, and `icxoi4hmq`. The complete [constituent ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/constituent-exposures.csv), [processing ledger](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/association-processing.csv), [verification result](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/lineage-result.json) and [manifest](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/header_followup/lineage-manifest.json) preserve the evidence and runnable extraction script.

These public images were processed in **June2026 with CALWF3 3.7.3**, and use square drizzle kernels, pixfrac1 and approximately0.12825arcsec pixels. The paper describes0.11arcsec pixels. Current archive products therefore are not a reconstruction of the published image recipe. `NLINCORR=COMPLETE` records the accumulated-count nonlinearity step; it does not prove that the separate count-rate response correction has been applied. STScI distinguishes these in its [pipeline calibration description](https://hst-docs.stsci.edu/wfc3dhb/chapter-3-wfc3-data-calibration/3-3-ir-data-calibration-steps) and [count-rate guidance](https://hst-docs.stsci.edu/wfc3dhb/chapter-7-wfc3-ir-sources-of-error/7-7-count-rate-non-linearity).

No measured flux, background, uncertainty or cosmological correction follows from the header inventory alone. The separate acquisition below now supplies the first/template F160W constituent files. Historical calibration references and the authors' alignment/subtraction/masking products remain unresolved.

## Current pixels acquired; conditional signed-aperture test completed

A separate [acquisition protocol](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_acquisition/protocol.json) selected the first F160W visit and the reference visit, their eight constituent FLTs, and two public DRZ products. All ten downloads completed, totaling158,296,320 bytes. An [independent structural review](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_acquisition/independent_validation/review.md) verifies their identities, sizes, local SHA256 hashes, exposure membership, HDUs, units and WCS. They contain no embedded FITS checksums. `fitsverify` reports no DRZ errors; each FLT's seven reported errors concern the HST-specific HDRLET extension type, with no other reported errors. This is a structural check, not an empirical calibration validation.

The official WFC3/IR pixel-area map and header-matched IDCTAB were also [acquired and checked independently](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_acquisition/pam_validation/review.md). On a predeclared968-point grid, the map and full TAN-SIP area Jacobians agree after common central normalization to median0.0096% and maximum0.078%; halving the derivative step changes the result by less than4.6e−9 fractionally. The official map, rather than a constant pixel area, can therefore be used in the conditional current-image experiment.

The [pixel-noise design](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_design/README.md) freezes separate source-mask and measurement exposures, signed aperture contrasts, complete annulus weights and a shared-template covariance check. A [pre-outcome amendment](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/pixel_design/masked-secondary-amendment.md) retains the strict primary while defining a secondary operator with explicit missing-pixel coverage. The [completed experiment](raisin-hst-signed-pixel-noise.md) finds zero strict positions and170 secondary positions. The secondary's repeat second moments are .708/.729 of the summed quoted diagonal variances; independent raw-pixel and statistical calculations agree. Error-model construction and omitted covariance remain unresolved, so these are conditional diagnostics. A blank-aperture test cannot by itself validate actual SN/host extraction or a count-rate correction.
