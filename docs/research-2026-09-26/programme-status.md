The current most informative avenue is E04/E07/E11: determine what the
repeatable band-dependent residual means. E02/E03/E09 controls run alongside
it because a fitted/selected population can create the same kind of signal.
The broad SED alternative makes E16 more urgent. E10 and corrected cosmology
remain downstream; choosing a preferred expansion history cannot supply the
missing calibration or population constraints.

The [active index](README.md) links the executed reports. Full-cohort nonlinear
SED compensation and a disjoint P21/G10 simulation holdout are complete.
The matched noise-variant control, selected derivative checks, retrospective
colour/shape-cut probe and optical-versus-infrared correction/overlap comparison
are complete within their stated scope. The observational baseline-noise audit
has reached a specific provenance/input gap, without scoring baseline flux.
BayeSN calibration/forward-model gates and the separate DES/BAO design are complete
within their stated scope. The immediate priority is the source-supported
RAISIN sign-selection issue: paired signed-epoch refits, explicit error-processing
controls, and simulations that distinguish a noise-dependent epoch cut from a
fixed observing cadence. The ten complete signed DES ancestors now support a
separate [optical-only BayeSN prediction experiment](bayesn-signed-optical-pilot.md),
conditional on the selected sample. Its optical-trigger timing, held-out NIR,
proper distance priors and dynamic forward adapter have explicit numerical
gates; missing survey selection limits interpretation rather than prohibiting
every observed conditional fit. The native paired fits now expose substantial numerical
starting-point sensitivity, independently verified in both modern and tagged
historical-source branches. A fixed-epoch, fixed-covariance native profile must
pass before those shifts can be interpreted as a measurement-selection response.
The first such native profile now passes independent numerical verification,
but reveals weakly distinguished template branches: its −.165mag preferred
point shift competes with a +.014mag response at almost the same objective.
The [profile decomposition](raisin-profile-decomposition.md) retains this
identification limit; the [ten-object extension](raisin-signed-cohort.md)
is complete and independently verified, including two exact-zero controls.
The [NIR timing audit](raisin-nir-inheritance-review.md) closes the immediate

The [identification review](identification-next-experiment.md) specifies which

The newly traced [CSP passband mismatch](csp-passband-lineage.md) adds a
concrete calibration test: original WIRC J photometry is numerically unchanged
while its downstream label selects RC1, although the original publication
associates WIRC with RC2. Independent observed NIR spectra and the
[completed stable all42 native comparison](csp-stable-filter-response.md)
now quantify the conditional label response. Its mean is +0.483432mmag;
this small mean does not bound unresolved physical-passband, training or
population effects. Existing simulation corrections and their
[required matched propagation](csp-filter-bias-propagation.md) are explicit.
Neither a template contrast nor an unpropagated distance response establishes
a cosmology change.

and reproduced the first eight historical fits. The source-matched population

timing comparison is stopped under that protocol. A prospective full-precision
conditional recovery and a separately justified treatment of quantization
uncertainty are being designed; neither is a repaired survey bias correction.
