# RAISIN WFC3/IR count-rate nonlinearity: inclusion audit

The released RAISIN HST magnitudes **cannot currently be classified as either corrected or uncorrected for count-rate nonlinearity (CRNL)**. The available author analysis code begins *after* image photometry: its input magnitude catalog and the executed image-reduction scripts/settings are missing. This is an execution-provenance gap, not evidence that the authors omitted a correction. The published HST calibration systematic is a wavelength-dependent zero-point variation, not an explicit source-rate correction.

This audit was scoped and frozen before inspecting calibration values in [`protocol.json`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/protocol.json). [`acquisition.json`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/acquisition.json) records verified Git blobs, and [`result.json`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/result.json) records the author-tree and released-file checks. No image pixels, native fits, or photometric corrections were made.

## What is established

- The [RAISIN paper, §2.2 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07801v2) describes FLT-image drizzling, template subtraction, a 0.4-arcsec aperture, aperture corrections checked with the CALSPEC star P330E, artificial-star/host-noise checks, and publicly available HST zero points from four white dwarfs and P330E. Its Appendix names `sndrizpipe`, `hstphot`, and `PythonPhot`, but does not identify executable commits, an image-photometry log, a CRNL term, or the reference count rates. The paper's calibration subsection attributes an approximately 0.5% F125W/F160W calibration uncertainty to the Bohlin et al. (2020) CALSPEC update. A missing keyword in the paper is **not** proof that the reduction lacked CRNL.
- The exact author `RAISIN_cosmo` tree at `b888214a5cbae38ac0bf886488ce7734f5b77e87` contains [`raisin_cosmo/update_phot.py`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/acquired/update_phot.py). It reads external `../phot/raisin_photometry.txt`, uses `diffmag_aper` and `diffmagerr_aper` to produce 27.5-zero-point FLUXCAL values, and optionally multiplies the flux/error by a template correction. It has no CRNL operation. The external magnitude catalog and its generating image-photometry code are absent from the pinned tree (29,596 blobs; searched path terms and null hits in `result.json`). Thus this source proves only absence **at the post-photometry conversion step**.
- For DES16E1dcx, the author's [`data/Photometry/DES_RAISIN/DES16E1dcx.snana.dat`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/acquired/DES16E1dcx.snana.dat) is byte-identical to the [released file](../../sources/repos/djones1040__RAISIN_DataRelease@a383c4b/photometry/RAISIN/DES_RAISIN/DES16E1dcx.snana.dat), SHA-256 `7c2054e0…c463`. Its six J/H FLUXCAL rows carry magnitudes converted upstream but no image count-rate, sky/background, CRNL flag, or magnitude-calibration provenance. This closes an author-to-release example, not the preceding pixel-to-magnitude lineage.
- The author `cosmo_sys.py` lines 203–220 defines `HST_CAL` as `MAGOBS_SHIFT_ZP_PARAMS 0 0.00714 0`. The three archived [`SUBMIT.INFO`](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_crnl_inclusion/acquired/DES_RAISIN-SUBMIT.INFO) files (DES, PS1, CSP) preserve it as `FITOPT002`. In local SNANA v11_03c `snana.car` lines 28790–28817, this evaluates to `0.00714 × observed filter mean wavelength [µm]` magnitudes. It varies by filter wavelength but has no count-rate or sky dependence. `cosmo_sys.py` lines 635–674 assigns it to the `hstcal` covariance group. The [release distance README](../../sources/repos/djones1040__RAISIN_DataRelease@a383c4b/distances/README.md) labels FITOPT002 an HST calibration uncertainty. This is evidence of a **generic HST calibration variation**, not an explicit CRNL model. It does not exclude a correction already embedded upstream in the nominal photometry.
- At independently pinned *current* source commits `srodney/hstphot@8e3b92f` and `djones1040/PythonPhot@7992eb6`, the inspected `hstphot/dophot.py`, `hstphot/hstzpt_apcorr.py`, and `PythonPhot/aper.py` have no CRNL/reciprocity operation. `dophot.py` lines 208–258 obtains a zero point, applies encircled-energy aperture corrections, and converts corrected flux to magnitude; `hstzpt_apcorr.py` lines 44–81 and 246 onward provide zero-point/aperture functions. These are **capability checks on pinned snapshots**, not evidence that those commits or those routines produced RAISIN's catalog. Their Git blob hashes are in `acquisition.json`.

The [STScI WFC3 photometry workflow](https://hst-docs.stsci.edu/wfc3dhb/chapter-9-wfc3-data-analysis/9-1-photometry) lists encircled-energy correction, magnitude zero point, and IR CRNL correction as *separate* steps. The [WFC3 Data Handbook §7.7](https://hst-docs.stsci.edu/wfc3dhb/chapter-7-wfc3-ir-sources-of-error/7-7-count-rate-non-linearity) states that CALWF3 does not apply the count-rate correction and describes an approximately 0.04-mag faint/sky-dominated source comparison with bright-standard zero points. [WFC3 ISR 2019-01](https://www.stsci.edu/files/live/sites/www/files/home/hst/instrumentation/wfc3/documentation/instrument-science-reports-isrs/_documents/2019/WFC3-2019-01.pdf) measures `0.0075 ± 0.0006 mag/dex` over a broad range, with no apparent wavelength dependence. That report explicitly distinguishes CRNL from total accumulated-count nonlinearity. CALWF3 `NLINCORR=COMPLETE` addresses the latter ([Handbook §7.6](https://hst-docs.stsci.edu/wfc3dhb/chapter-7-wfc3-ir-sources-of-error/7-6-detector-nonlinearity-issues)). Current MAST primary headers examined separately show `NLINCORR=COMPLETE` but were reprocessed in 2026; they establish neither a 2016 reduction version nor RAISIN CRNL treatment. The nominal SN effect cannot be inferred from the general ~0.04-mag illustration: effective local total (SN plus sky) rates and the calibration reference/pivot matter.

## Missing execution link

To classify inclusion, obtain the exact RAISIN `../phot/raisin_photometry.txt` provenance, image-photometry script/command and version, calibration zero-point/reference files and their epoch, an explicit correction/log if used, and per-exposure SN, local sky and standard-star count-rate convention. The source must connect each J/H magnitude to its input image and the released SNANA row. P330E aperture checks, Bohlin zeropoints, a 0.5% CALSPEC uncertainty, FITOPT002, and a CALWF3 `NLINCORR` header cannot individually supply this link.

No shared 0.04-mag offset, differential distance shift, or cosmology implication is established by this audit.

The independent [physical-response review](../../runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/raisin_crnl_review/README.md) derives the sign, source-plus-background response and faint-background limit; specifies when a calibration convention could absorb the effect; and distinguishes rate response from2025–2026 accumulated-count recalibration. Its synthetic checks validate algebra only. The next calibration test must establish the effective target/standard illumination and the executed photometry convention before applying a correction.
