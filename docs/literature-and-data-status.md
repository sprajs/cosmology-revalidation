The ZTF photometry is a useful independent-survey lead, but its G26 training connects to Foundation/CSP/CfA/PS1/SDSS/SNLS/DES. A normalized-name audit finds three Pantheon aliases in the linked ZTF master list, only one with a lite-archive light curve; none by the same literal-name procedure in Dovekie. ## Directional branch and subsequent correction

[Sah et al., 2606.09650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09650) is examined separately from isotropic CPL: full latent light-curve C2 and corrected/reversed-bias C1, multiple frames, a decaying dipole and a Son-type age shift. The public C2 covariance was initially missing locally and then recovered from its linked Zenodo record; this barrier is resolved. Published journal methods and appendix were recovered and checked. See the [directional report](investigations/directional-audit.md) for final likelihood-level reproduction and limits.

The original dossier missed [Sah's 3 August response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02484) and the **3 August v2** of [Ray, 2607.20570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20570v2). Ray v2 changes the title to accelerating expansion, acknowledges a Galactic/Equatorial coordinate error and withdraws its original strong deceleration values. It also states it cannot reproduce its original full-sample baseline: the coordinate error cannot explain that reversal alone. Its simplified equal-error, fixed-jerk likelihood is not Sah's C2 likelihood. Historical and revised sources are distinguished and preserved; the obsolete v1 result is not used as current evidence.

## Source and code preservation

Original acquisitions are never refreshed in place. Initial URLs/hashes/commits remain under `catalog/`, `papers/`, `data/` and `sources/repos/`. Later source-specific records live under `runs/age_signal/acquisition.json`, `runs/mapping/sources-2026-09-20/`, `sources/updates/2026-09-20-standardization/`, `sources/updates/2026-09-20-ztf/` and `runs/directional/sources/`. The [reproducibility guide](reproducibility.md) gives the authoritative calculation order and retention policy. A remote mutable URL is not a substitute for its local recorded digest.

The primary-source checks correct the acquisition dossier in consequential ways: G11/R19 are overlapping; a small explicit mass-step parameter does not imply absent host corrections; modern matrices and table errors have different meanings; the published Chung mock differs from its arXiv text; Ray's current conclusion differs from the originally acquired version; and September dust/calibration/host papers add relevant evidence. These corrections do not make every remaining author result exactly reproducible.
