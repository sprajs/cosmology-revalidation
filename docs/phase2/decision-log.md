# Phase 2 decisions and amendments

## 20–21 September: observation-level preparation, before population outcomes

The root plan and candidate model/priors were recorded before real-data inference. Literature/source and full-catalogue audits proceed in parallel with an official-fitter reproduction. Real correction ranking remains gated on assessment of that baseline.

The metadata-covariance audit found both rounding loss and a known MINOS-versus-parabolic-error convention mismatch. The official fitter is therefore built locally and patched only to export its actual double-precision parameters and full Hessian covariance; the scientific fitting code is unchanged by those output patches. Positivity failures in a naively reconstructed summary covariance are not called proof that the original scientific likelihood was invalid.

Current SNANA approximately reproduces most fits, but substantial outliers remain. A historical executable was also built and tested rather than assuming current code was equivalent. Public accepted-observation records identify PHOTFLAG bit32 handling as a concrete configuration lead. No rows are removed merely to improve reproduction agreement. Raw duplicate-exposure groups accepted in some published fits are separately investigated; a flag is not proof of a cosmological bias.

Published reference simulations have deterministic pre-dust luminosity conditional on their stored latent parameters and no independent coherent scatter. Relative importance reweighting cannot safely span arbitrary luminosity shifts on that singular conditional support. New forward simulations using published G10/BS21/P21 prescriptions, generated denominators and brightness perturbations are being prepared. The released W22 population table additionally requires an age-bearing host library; replacing that missing input by an invented age proxy would not be an exact W22 run.

## 21 September: synthetic engine performance amendment

Independent adaptive-quadrature convolution, selected-normalization and constant-q distance tests passed before any DES hierarchical fit. The first 1,000-object selected synthetic NUTS run used generic batched 3x3 linear algebra and diagonal mass adaptation; it completed in 352.6 seconds just before an attempted interruption (the process was already gone). It is a successful computational comparison, not a failed or interrupted fit. Its configuration/log/source snapshots and numerical posterior are retained under `phase2/hierarchy/performance-pilots/`. Replace tiny-matrix dispatch with explicit Cholesky algebra and use dense mass adaptation for correlated global parameters. The independent numerical checks passed again before the faster recovery replay. This changes computational implementation and sampler adaptation, not the population law, priors, selection law or scientific acceptance criterion.

The replay completed in 81.5 seconds with no divergences and effective sample sizes above 1,800. Its posterior agrees with the original sampler. One dust-scale parameter differs from its generating value by roughly 2.5 standard deviations; a single realization is not a coverage test. Before interpreting this as either bias or chance, freeze 30 repeated independent MLE/Hessian recovery checks and four seeds each of free-q recovery, incorrectly omitted selection, same-variance heavy-tail luminosity noise, equal-mean non-exponential dust, and unmodelled 0.30*z/(1+z) luminosity drift. These are diagnostic synthetic counterexamples, not evidence that any misspecified law describes DES. Two optimization starts and Hessian/gradient/boundary diagnostics are retained. This is not called Bayesian simulation-based calibration.

## 21 September: user-directed deeper assumption audit

Use every available concurrent Astra slot and subsequent waves to test SNANA's assumptions, not only its outputs. Separate the empirical calibration of an assumption, numerical verification of its implementation, an observation-level falsification test, and an unidentifiable premise. Expand the dependency register through images/scene modelling, photometric calibration, flux noise/covariance, spectral surfaces and colour law, redshift/extinction, fitter priors/bounds/clipping, population/selection and cosmological interpretation. A simulation generated and analysed under one shared law does not validate that law empirically. Published calibrated flux is not raw detector counts; record the lower-level public-data boundary explicitly.

## 21 September: assess the baseline before conditional prediction

The author-accepted-epoch arm accounts for all 62,702 published accepted DES records without altering their flux or deduplicating them. This conditions on the authors' rejection decisions; it does not reproduce the hidden original clipping process. The subsequent finite-multistart arm retains every object and selects the lowest reported objective among attempted starts rather than selecting the solution closest to publication. DES1307748 has two materially different light-curve modes. The high-stretch mode remains better under either common frozen covariance, so agreement cannot honestly be forced by choosing the published-like mode. It is retained and explicitly identified for multimodality sensitivity before held-out population outcomes are opened.

The independent flux calculation validates the pilot means and most compressed parameter curvature much more closely than the whole 4x4 Hessian. One peak-time Hessian error is too small by about 4.55 relative to both MINOS and an independently scanned likelihood profile. Across the pilot's mB/x1/c covariance blocks, generalized eigenvalues span approximately 0.946–1.200. Positive definiteness alone was not sufficient. The actual exported matrices remain unchanged; their approximation is now tested rather than assumed.

The broad original calibrated-flux reconstruction is therefore characterized but not exact. The separately preregistered conditional-prediction benchmark may test empirical relations in these pre-BBC observables with that explicit qualification; it may not claim a regenerated official pipeline, physical correction identification, or selection-free acceleration. The original independent hierarchy still refuses an unvalidated real survey selector. Exact published-mask, recovered-mask and multimodal-fit dependence must remain visible.

Nine forward variants have each generated 26,518 attempts. All source/output hashes and generated denominators pass their audits. Their redshift, LIBID, host ID, peak time and sky coordinates coincide by generated-attempt index. This establishes common design coordinates, not identical population/noise draws. Reused CIDs on early failures are never used as unique denominator keys. Measured-flux fitting and basic-quality selection are running for every variant before model-comparison summaries are assembled.
