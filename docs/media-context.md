The video sequence is a useful route into the literature but is no longer the whole story: the final published counter-response includes material beyond the May preprint, and a July correction exists for Paper I. The November video cannot provide context for later papers.

The reporting contains several shortcuts to avoid in future work: an age-correlation significance is not the significance of cosmic deceleration; evidence against constant `w = -1` is not proof of no dark energy; “mass correction” includes assumptions and can interact with bias corrections; and agreement between papers using the same SNe and age prescription is not fully independent replication.
