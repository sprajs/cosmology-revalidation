# SALT and SNANA dust audit

Opened 2026-09-21 alongside the active observation-level reanalysis. This audit owns `docs/salt-dust-audit/`, `scripts/salt_dust_audit/` and `runs/salt_dust_audit/`. Existing source inputs and `phase2/` work are read-only to this team. No public communication or source publication is authorized by this audit.

The question is whether the available observations validate the assumptions that transport fitted supernova brightness into distance. Agreement with an assumed simulator does not prove that its dust population describes the Universe. A finite suite cannot certify absence of every bias. The alternative deliverable requested by the user is an explicit record of measured implementation errors, conditional sensitivities, propagated uncertainty matrices, collinearities, and unresolved identification limits.

Four concurrent investigations:

1. SALT2/SALT3 training, colour law, model dimensionality, covariance, calibration, population support and exact latent degeneracies, checked against primary sources and the actual local model files.
2. SNANA Galactic and host dust implementation, historical and current extinction laws, simulation population and BBC/selection configuration, with executable numerical comparisons.
3. Released DES and Dovekie systematic matrices, row ordering and covariance/precision semantics, uncertainty modes, cosmological projection and confounding. Covariance eigenvectors do not uniquely recover signed correction vectors.
4. Independent broadband SALT response at observed DES cadence, passbands and errors. Compute the matrix mapping physical dust and calibration perturbations into fitted parameters and standardized brightness. This is a conditional local response, not a new selection-corrected distance catalogue.

The fourth experiment is specified before inspecting its numerical outcomes. Select up to 64 equally spaced ranks in redshift from the original DES Hubble members with published accepted epochs. At published SALT coordinates, use the released SALT3.DES5YR surface with `sncosmo` and F99 propagation. Retain accepted epochs; do not reselect them after injections. Fit the local brightness/stretch/colour/time tangent space to host E(B−V) perturbations at several fixed R_V, Galactic E(B−V), and four independent passband zeropoints. Compare measurement-only and measurement-plus-SALT-model covariance. Preserve the actual Jacobian, response operator, nuisance Gram matrices, residual information, parameter covariance and object IDs. Test finite-difference stability and nonlinear injected-flux recovery. No nuisance prior is invented from these arbitrary unit perturbations, no eigenvector signs are treated as physical signs, and no fitted SALT c is labelled a direct E(B−V) measurement.

Synthesis must distinguish (a) corrected implementation issues, (b) quantified conditional uncertainty, (c) observable inadequacy of a model, (d) structural nonidentifiability, and (e) unavailable empirical constraints. The audit is complete only after scripts, matrices, sources, limitations and reproduction commands have been independently checked; it will not claim a bias-free cosmological result.

Implementation amendment: the first 12-object pilot completed numerical work but failed final manifest serialization on a relative path. Its model/data ratio exposed omitted SNANA `MAG_OFFSET=.27` and KCOR primary magnitudes. HEAD MWEBV was also compared with full-precision SNANA output and already contains the map rescaling; multiplying it by .86 again was removed. The corrected pilot is rerun before the registered 64-object calculation. These are errors in this new diagnostic, not newly discovered errors in the published DES analysis.
