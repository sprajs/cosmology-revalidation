## A2: Correction factorial and regression (confirmatory reproduction plus sensitivity)

Question: How do published Pantheon corrections alter the same sample's age slope? Primary residual y = MU_SH0ES - mu_LCDM(zHD,zHEL), flat Omega_m=.3; free intercept absorbs absolute zero point. Corrected vs correction removed y + biasCor_m_b, justified by inspecting release definitions and comparing to Tripp alpha=.148 beta=3.112. Before calling this a pure mass correction, assess the released correction's content. Use published HR errors; alternate subtract COVADD only if variance units are confirmed. Compare full, no-bias, and no-bias/no-intrinsic, full and narrow z ranges, without changing more than one factor at a time. Fit WLS (diagnostic, ignores age errors) plus LINMIX if reproducibly available; otherwise normal-latent-age errors-in-variables likelihood with explicit age-distribution assumptions, labelled approximate. Include free residual intrinsic variance rather than assuming unexplained variance vanishes when an error term is removed. Test Omega_m=.2/.4 and both z frames, overlap policies, full covariance and redshift/mass/colour joint models where support permits. Do not interpret an insignificant slope as proof of no luminosity evolution; do not interpret an uncorrected correlation as an extra correction for corrected distances.

## A4: Controlled mock audit (new discriminating method test, pre-registered)

Generate paired sets of 200 galaxies per redshift bin in .05 intervals from .00 to .40, age means decreasing with z, fixed age scatter, injected slope -.034 mag/Gyr, Gaussian noise .12 mag; seed 20260920 and 1000 realizations. Explicit synthetic age distributions are a controlled surrogate, never author inputs. Cases: (i) physical magnitudes + exactly correct LCDM baseline, no centering; (ii) same magnitudes, bin-centered residuals as stated in C26; (iii) both x and y bin-demeaned, estimating within-bin slope; (iv) null age signal. Predict (i) unbiased full slope, (ii) b_hat = b * within-age variance / total-age variance in noiseless OLS, (iii) unbiased slope if within-bin age information remains, (iv) null. Vary between/within age variation; derive identity analytically and numerically. Report recovery and false-positive rates with Monte Carlo errors. A reference LCDM subtraction alone does not algebraically enforce constant mean HR; fitted distances can project out components degenerate with fitted cosmology and this must be modelled explicitly.
