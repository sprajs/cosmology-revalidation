## What objective is preserved

`FCNCHI2_PRIOR` uses `PEAKMJD-INIVAL(IPAR_PEAKMJD)`. The local optimizer start leaves that center unchanged. With the same nominal NML and pre-hook state in all arms, the **first-iteration objective function** is identical. Do not reuse the previously failed NML−2 file, which changed initialization before this hook.

ITER1 is not a frozen-covariance Gaussian objective: it uses parameter-dependent model-error variance in its diagonal chi-square. Therefore W evaluated at different starting/final coordinates can differ while the objective function is unchanged. The valid comparisons are the unchanged pre-hook masks/fudge/covariance initialization and prior center, or objective/mean/variance at the same coordinates. Demanding equality of final ITER1 W across shifted trajectories would be an incorrect extra gate.

Later iterations natively rebuild covariance from preceding fits and recenter the peak prior. Different trajectories can consequently have different later objective functions. This experiment tests stability of that full iterative estimator from genuinely different initial optimizer positions. It does not establish global minimization of one immutable likelihood, calibrated uncertainty, or a survey timing-bias correction. Retain all original support, row, truth, boundary, 9-versus12-iteration and full null-identity gates; no gate is relaxed here.
