# Conditional covariance and generator-noise audit

The frozen same-support diagnostic reproduces all original observer scores; it changes neither input covariance nor selected epochs. The main comparison is archived basic quality plus the NEW common measured-input classifier, strict pIa>.999. This does not claim recovery of released probabilities or real-sample purity.

The additional frozen SNR15 split retains530 real objects and gives .901160, .688512, .675755 respectively. Pilot weights have effective sample sizes81.40/71.24 for P21/G10 in the31-cell comparison, and69.88/62.50 after SNR matching. The discrepancy persists after this limited support matching; unsupported cells are not extrapolated.

Runtime NML echoes in all five nominal native fits have FUDGE_MAG_COVERR blank and OPT_COVAR_MWXTERR=1. In SNANA-audit-v3/src/snlc_fit.F90:10701–10759 the total is SALT model covariance plus local MW outer product plus diagonal quoted-data/fudge variance. COVFUDGE_DATA returns zero for the blank runtime string (14853–14893). The exported PHASE2_COVSET five columns store MW flux error, model magnitude error, previous-iteration model flux, data flux error and fudge flux error. The diagonal source expression is evaluated in R4 arithmetic; the checker reproduces its single-precision square/sum before converting to double. Every saved fudge-flux-error entry is zero.

Across2040 object/law cases, the isolated whitened SALT remainder's minimum eigenvalue is -1.27e-16, numerical zero; no positivity repair was applied. C is the exported fitting iteration's covariance, not newly recomputed from the final mean. The real sample has a larger relative fitted SALT contribution, so “the simulations merely received more SALT covariance fraction” does not explain these numbers. The true scatter may nevertheless differ between generated populations and real data.

The historical generator has distinct physical and measurement routes:

* `SNANA-2fe0f56/src/snlc_sim.c:23959–24185` builds measurement noise from source/host photon statistics, sky/read noise, zeropoint error and optional reference-template noise. SMEARFLAG_ZEROPT=3 applies its fluctuation and reports its marginal error. It is not the shared survey calibration prior.
* `snlc_sim.c:24276–24336` calls get_FLUXERRMODEL to obtain separate true and reported uncertainty maps. In the nominal map those scaling routes match; the extra true variance is carried as TYPE_FLUXNOISE_F. Existing declared noisetrue alternatives deliberately change that relation.
* The actual DES-SN5YR_DES_FLUXERRMODEL_SIM.DAT lines22–23 declares REDCOV=.6 separately for each griz band in shallow/deep fields. Both archived nominal generation logs explicitly print all eight .600 groups. `snlc_sim.c:24446–24565` creates the within-object extra-noise covariance with diagonal sigma_F² and offdiagonal .6 sigma_F_i sigma_F_j; a Cholesky draw supplies correlated flux shifts. This coefficient does NOT correlate the entire quoted noise or different supernovae. Optional template noise has its own same-band correlation.
* `snlc_sim.c:24643–24700` adds search/zeropoint, reference-template and extra-noise draws to flux, then adjusts the reported photon variance using observed flux. Quoted variances can therefore depend on the noise realization. Native fitting's diagonal data term does not reconstruct those generated offdiagonal photometric correlations. SALT error-map covariance is an additional distinct predictive term, and need not equal the missing photometric covariance.
* The P21 nominal generation log explicitly initializes intrinsic smearing model=NONE; G10 initializes G10 wavelength scatter. P21 still draws population/dust/beta variation. GENMODEL_ERRSCALE defaults to0 (`snlc_sim.c:922`) and neither relevant input nor GENPDF overrides it; there is no independent draw of the fitter's SALT covariance as its own residual process. The obsolete positive-ERRSCALE branch is guarded at25999–26034. Thus fitting-C and generated residual covariance are not expected to agree by construction.

These source facts provide concrete mechanisms for non-unit projected chi-square, especially after nuisance fitting, clipping and classifier selection. They do not prove which mechanism causes the observed .69/.90 gap, that any applied correction is erroneous, or that a physical random term is counted twice. No covariance term is subtracted, and no uncertainty is rescaled.

The strongest next controlled comparison is either a source-gated paired use of existing P21 rho000/rho090/noisetrue120 epoch simulations, or an independently seeded regeneration with explicit covariance/selection bookkeeping. Existing variants must first be joined by generated-attempt identity and verified latent/cadence coordinates; reused CID alone is insufficient. The frozen full archived-fit holdout proceeds unchanged. Any bootstrap from one stored catalogue describes finite-catalogue variability, not a calibrated physical-null distribution or a posterior probability of new bias.
