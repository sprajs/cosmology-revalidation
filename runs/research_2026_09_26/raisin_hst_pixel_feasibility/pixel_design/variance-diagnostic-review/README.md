# Pre-outcome review: same-data ERR and aperture variance diagnostics

2026-09-26. Source/theory only; no SCI, ERR, DQ values or aperture outcomes inspected. Add the descriptive quantities below to the existing fixed branches. There is no new eligibility gate, tuning parameter, multiple-testing family, correction or data-dependent noise rescaling. The strict primary and masked secondary remain distinct.

## Why standardized residuals alone cannot identify a noise-scale error

For one fixed paired aperture contrast write `d=μ+ε`. Its reported variance `Vhat` can depend on the same ramp/signal that produced `ε`. Even if `E[Vhat]=Var(ε)`, it does not follow that `E[d²/Vhat]=1`, or that `d/sqrt(Vhat)` is Gaussian. This is a matter of statistical dependence, independent of any detector defect. WFC3's final FLT ERR is an estimated native ramp-fit uncertainty, not a known independent variance draw.

An analytic counterexample makes the distinction exact. Let `Z~N(0,1)`, `d=σZ`, and `Vhat=σ² exp(aZ−a²/2)`. The variance estimate is positive and unbiased: `E[Vhat]=σ²`. Nevertheless,

`E[d²/Vhat]=(1+a²) exp(a²)`,

while `E[d²−Vhat]=0`. This is a mathematical illustration of endogeneity, **not a proposed WFC3 error model**. Different relationships between Vhat and d can change the standardized moment in either direction. No conclusion follows from the direction of this example alone.

## Recommended additive output

For every already-declared branch/visit and its existing spatial/background summaries, retain

- `N`, `sum_d=Σd_j`, `sum_d2=Σd_j²`;
- `sum_V=ΣVhat_j`;
- `excess_second_moment=Σ(d_j²−Vhat_j)`;
- `pooled_second_moment_ratio=Σd_j²/ΣVhat_j`;
- the existing `Σd_j²/Vhat_j` and signed standardized summaries, clearly labelled descriptive.

Use the exact same positions, masks, units and covariance comparator. No weights inverse to a measured variance enter the first five sums. Compute in float64 and preserve the per-position d,V ledger. Do not winsorize, clip, rescale uncertainties or select a favorable ratio. The pooled ratio is the ratio of two random sums; it is **not exactly unbiased in a finite sample**. Its useful property is that it is generated by the estimating equation `Σ(d²−Vhat)=0`, which avoids the reciprocal-variance endogeneity in the individually standardized statistic. Heterogeneous high-variance positions contribute more, so it answers a different, explicitly pooled question.

## Exact expectation and assumptions

Let `G` denote the frozen design information and the conditioning used to define the selected operator. The identity

`E[d_j²−Vhat_j | G] = (E[d_j|G])² + Var(d_j|G) − E[Vhat_j|G]`

needs no independence between d and Vhat. Therefore the excess moment has expectation zero if the paired mean is zero and quoted V is unbiased for the **actual selected contrast variance**, conditional on G. Unbiasedness before selection is insufficient. No independence between different positions is needed for this expectation, but spatial dependence controls its uncertainty.

For native weights `w2,w4` and pixel covariance blocks `C22,C44,C24`,

`Var(d|G)=w2ᵀ C22 w2 + w4ᵀ C44 w4 −2 w2ᵀ C24 w4`.

The proposed comparator uses only within-image diagonal ERR² and omits C24. Cross-pixel correlations, shared backgrounds/read patterns, persistent detector effects, common flat/reference uncertainties and unequal source responses can all affect the moment. A positive excess is not uniquely an underestimated ERR; a negative excess is not uniquely an overestimated noise scale. For example, a shared error can cancel from the difference although summing two marginal error variances would count it twice. Shared flat errors need not cancel fully when dithers and aperture weights differ.

The weights include background-annulus subtraction. That propagates the quoted random annulus contribution but not an unknown coherent background or host-gradient mismatch. The PHOTFLAM scaling is a fixed convention change, not uncertainty calibration. The masked secondary has additional unequal-response terms from missing pixels; static-scene residuals can enter its paired mean and second moment.

The source mask is determined on other exposures, which avoids directly selecting test residuals. It does not prove independence when detector/calibration errors persist across exposures. DQ/finite/positive-ERR eligibility and error-derived masks remain data-dependent; the statistics concern that accepted population. The conditional-unbiasedness assumption must be stated, not asserted from the mere presence of ERR.

## Centering and uncertainty

Keep raw signed means alongside the uncentered moments: a real pair offset contributes μ². The existing fitted-intercept statistic is useful descriptively, but inverse-V fitting reintroduces the same endogeneity, and `N−1` is not a certified chi-square degree of freedom.

Even unweighted centering is not a universal fix. With contrast vector d of mean μ and spatial covariance C,

`E[Σ(d−mean(d))²] = tr(C)−1ᵀC1/N + Σ(μ−mean(μ))²`.

Subtracting `(1−1/N)ΣVhat` leaves off-diagonal covariance and any spatially varying mean. Do not silently apply an independence-based degrees-of-freedom correction to obtain a calibrated variance estimate. This review recommends no new centered statistic beyond those already frozen.

Use the predeclared tile jackknife/bootstrap descriptively for both raw sums and the ratio, recomputing all sums inside each resample. Preserve spatial maps and background strata. Two visits and one field cannot establish the distribution of global background/flat/calibration errors, or turn a tile interval into a physical-noise p-value. A block uncertainty smaller than a field-wide systematic is not evidence that the latter is absent. No standard-normal tail interpretation or Poisson-error retuning is added.
