# Frozen executor details before Stage A

This implements `pixel_design/protocol.json` and its two pre-outcome amendments: `masked-secondary-amendment.json` and `variance-diagnostic-review/amendment.json`. The design parameters, selection thresholds, exposure split, and Stage A/Stage B release boundary are controlling. `input-freeze.json` pins all eight current FLTs, official PAM and IDCTAB, design files, resource addendum, and both executor source files. `run.py` refuses any hash or size mismatch.

Stage A reads only headers, official PAM, design exposures 1 and 3 SCI/ERR/DQ, and test exposures 2 and 4 ERR/DQ plus SCI finiteness. It produces fixed masks, sparse fractional-overlap coefficients and per-candidate strict/secondary quality coverage. It does not sum test SCI. Stage B requires root's explicit JSON release referencing the completed Stage A hash and a separately chosen positive Stage B seconds cap.

Implementation details fixed before support counts:

- Gnomonic east/north tangent coordinates centered on RA 8.9685°, Dec −43.35812°; 6-arcsec integer lattice including the fixed origin, then SN 5-arcsec exclusion. Each 2-arcsec annulus is sampled at 64 sky angles and must lie at least two native pixels inside every FLT. The common WCS-only tangent bounding rectangle defines sixteen 4×4 spatial tiles. The bounds are not adjusted after masks.
- Design background uses the median of finite SCI, positive finite ERR, DQ0 pixels in each detector-origin 64×64 tile. Empty-validity tiles take background zero and their 3×3 boxes are ineligible for source detection. A 3×3 box is eligible only if all nine pixels satisfy that same validity rule; the absolute box residual must exceed five times the square root of its summed ERR². Invalid boxes are counted but **not** dilated as astrophysical sources. Their DQ/ERR/finite status enters strict or secondary support separately. This makes the policy shared across branches without treating bad DQ as a detected source.
- A flagged source pixel is mapped as its full four-corner TAN-SIP sky quadrilateral. A lattice center is excluded if its minimum distance to the union is ≤2.4 arcsec. This implements dilation of pixels, not just their centers.
- Native circle/annulus overlaps use exact TAN-SIP mapping of each pixel's four corners to the tangent plane and bilinear subpixel interpolation. A pre-score, fixed 11×11 detector grid with 3×3 within-pixel offsets compares bilinear to direct SIP and must differ by ≤1e−4 native pixel. Quadrature starts 16×16 and doubles to 32×32; if flux, variance, aperture and annulus synthetic integrals do not agree to 0.1%, it deterministically tries 32/64 and 64/128, then stops. No observed flux chooses resolution. Official PAM is indexed directly `[y−1,x−1]`; all FLTs are full-frame, LTV0/LTM1.
- For each geometry vector, synthetic constant SCI cancellation, signed pulse recovery, direct background subtraction versus assembled weights, κ/PAM scaling, and diagonal ERR² variance are checked before any held-out aperture sum. All eight exposure coverage fractions and rejection reasons are recorded. The strict branch requires full valid support; the masked secondary uses the separate inclusive 0.90/0.75 area gates on all eight exposures and retained-area annulus coefficient. There is no aperture coverage renormalization.
- A geometry, quality-numerical, or synthetic-operator failure is a **Stage A stop**, not a candidate rejection. The failing candidate and reason, completed rows and sparse operators are saved in `stage_a_partial.json` and `operators.partial.npz`; no Stage B release can use a partial Stage A. Signed BITPIX16 DQ is decoded through uint16 for descriptive bit counts. DQ==0 eligibility itself is unchanged.
- To enforce the 100 MB newly allocated working-array cap, retained sparse operator fields stop at 50,000,000 bytes. Source-mask/background planes contribute about 20 MB, and quadrature samples are processed eight boundary pixels at a time (about 2 MB for the two-coordinate sample at 128×128, with bounded arithmetic intermediates). NPZ fields are concatenated and written one at a time. Exceeding the field cap stops with partial output; it does not thin candidates. Memory-mapped FITS inputs are pre-existing files, not newly allocated analysis arrays.
- If Stage A reaches 120 seconds, it writes the completed candidate/support ledger and mask hash, then stops. Root reviews timing/support before a separately capped Stage B release. Stage B writes its main signed ledgers and a primary checkpoint before dependent radius/reversal sensitivities; any later failure preserves that checkpoint. Stopped branches (<30 positions) are not scored. Spatial bootstrap/delete-one results are withheld if fewer than 12 tiles or any occupied tile has fewer than three positions; no position is dropped to create resampling support.
- Radius 0.2/0.8 arcsec diagnostics use the same original strict cohort and source mask, with radius-specific invalid-pixel losses ledgered. Reversal scores exposures 1 and 3 on the **original** source mask; it is dependent and explicitly not a newly held-out sample. The masked secondary gets neither extra sensitivity. All branches report raw `Σd`, `Σd²`, `ΣV`, `Σ(d²−V)` and `Σd²/ΣV` as descriptive moments, retaining that the ratio is a random-denominator statistic.

No pixel-derived magnitude, detector correction, physical-null p-value or historical RAISIN equivalence is implied by this executor.
