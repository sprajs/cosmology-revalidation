# Independent review of the fixed flat-reference variance component

The parent calculation is a read-only source-derived decomposition of the **existing quoted FLT ERR**, evaluated at the already frozen 170 masked-secondary positions in all eight F160W images. This review independently read those same archived operators, public FLTs, and named PFLT/DFLT reference files. It did not change SCI, ERR, the pixel weights, support membership, or any noise score.

All science arrays are 1014×1014 with `LTV1=LTV2=0` and unit `LTM1_1=LTM2_2`; the PFLT/DFLT arrays are 1024×1024 with `LTV1=LTV2=+5` and unit LTM. Thus science zero-based `(x,y)` uses reference zero-based `(x+5,y+5)`, covering reference indices 5–1018. Every one of the 1,360 exposure operators passed this offset and in-bounds check. The same PFLT `4ac1921li_pfl.fits` is named in all eight FLTs; search DFLT is `4ac1822ni_dfl.fits`, template DFLT `4ac1831ei_dfl.fits`. The FITS header has `LFLTFILE=N/A`. In pinned CALWF3 3.7.3 source, [`refdata.c`](../../source-3.7.3/refdata.c) lines 191–216 multiplies PFLT and DFLT, and [`flatcorr.c`](../../source-3.7.3/flatcorr.c) lines 41–58 and 108–109 divides science by the combined flat. [`math.c`](../../source-3.7.3/math.c) lines 329–335, 370–380 propagates diagonal product and quotient errors. Algebraically the relative combined-flat variance is `(σ_P/P)²+(σ_D/D)²`, contributing `(y·σ_F/F)²` to each already corrected pixel's quoted variance. This is the source's *diagonal propagation convention*, not evidence that the actual reference errors lack cross-pixel correlation.

Independently reconstructing the fixed masked coefficient `w_j=κv_j[a_jPAM_j−(ΣvaPAM/Σvb)b_j]` gave exposure flat component `Σ_j(w_jy_j)²τ_j²`, with `τ_j²=(σ_P/P)²+(σ_D/D)²`. Joining the two within-visit exposures by identical detector pixel gave the shared-reference term `Σ_j(w₂jy₂j)(w₄jy₄j)τ_j²`. The review checked all 1,360 exposure rows, 340 pairs and both pooled summaries against the parent result. The maximum absolute arithmetic difference was `2.84×10⁻¹⁴` (pooled quoted-V summation); per-exposure flat terms matched to `1.36×10⁻²⁰` and pair flat covariance to `1.17×10⁻²¹`.

These are only the PFLT/DFLT terms present under the source's diagonal-reference rule and same-detector-pixel reuse. The reference files do not supply cross-pixel or PFLT–DFLT covariance, and this computation neither measures such terms nor asserts a corrected total contrast variance. In particular, the small identified flat term cannot be treated as a complete explanation for the current-image repeat statistic.

The SAMP/TIME audit covers the exact valid pixels in the frozen operators, without looking at their signed contrast outcomes. Across 838,539 operator-pixel occurrences, 836,084 (99.707%) have SAMP=8, 2,452 have SAMP=7, two have SAMP=6 and one has SAMP=3. All headers say `NSAMP=8`, `SAMP_SEQ=SPARS50`, `EXPTIME≈302.935 s`; valid-pixel TIME is usually the full exposure, with minima ≈252.934, 202.934, or 52.933 s in the reduced-SAMP strata. The seven-point, 50-s synthetic `linfit` calculation is therefore aligned with the dominant full-duration metadata *if* those pixels had one unsplit accepted fit interval. SAMP/TIME alone cannot establish that interval, the exact accepted read list, or the SNR-selected power; the small reduced-support strata are not covered by a single universal seven-read formula. No branch-specific real-pixel variance calculation was added.

`protocol.json` froze the inputs and tolerances before independent recomputation; `execution-freeze.json` pins the script; `result.json` contains per-exposure support and maximum arithmetic gaps. The parent output remains unchanged in `../result.json`.
