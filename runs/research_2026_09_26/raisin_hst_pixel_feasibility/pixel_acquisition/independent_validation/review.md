# Independent FITS acquisition validation

All ten public files match the parent acquisition's exact MAST URI, HTTP size, per-file sidecar, full-file SHA-256 and expected name. Total: 158,296,320 bytes. Each DRZ primary header identifies exactly its four acquired FLT `SCI,1` inputs, with no duplicate or cross-association member. See `result.json` for each file's complete metadata and hash; `protocol.json` and `prescan-hashes.txt` preceded validation.

The two DRZ FITS files pass `fitsverify -e` with zero errors. Each of the eight FLTs gives exactly seven errors solely because `fitsverify` does not register HST's `HDRLET` extension type; all seven correspond to the seven archived alternate-WCS headerlets. `fitsverify-qualification.json` retains these lines. This is a FITS-convention compatibility qualification, not a claim that the binary file is corrupt. None of the HDUs has an embedded `CHECKSUM` or `DATASUM`, so an embedded FITS checksum cannot be verified. Full local SHA-256 closes to the downloaded file identity reported by the acquisition ledger, not to a separately published MAST digest.

Each FLT has `PRIMARY`, 1014×1014 `SCI` and `ERR` in `ELECTRONS/S`, `DQ` marked `UNITLESS`, `SAMP`, `TIME`, `WCSCORR`, and seven `HDRLET` extensions. The two DRZ products have `PRIMARY`, `SCI`, `WHT`, `CTX`, and four-row `HDRTAB`; `SCI` is `ELECTRONS/S`, while `WHT` and `CTX` are marked `UNITLESS`. The DRZ headers record `NDRIZIM=4`, square kernel, pixfrac 1, and scale 0.1282500 arcsec/pixel. Their four individual final-mask names are *references*, not among the ten acquired files. Nothing in this audit treats the WHT plane as an inverse-variance map or interprets DQ bits.

FLT `SCI` headers contain TAN-SIP WCS with fourth-order SIP distortion coefficients and embedded alternate-WCS headerlets; DRZ `SCI` headers contain final TAN WCS. Primary headers reference `iref$w3m18525i_idc.fits` distortion and `iref$4ac1921li_pfl.fits` flat; these reference files and a pixel-area-map realization were not acquired. `PAMFILE` is absent. Thus the current image headers carry usable declared coordinate mappings, while an independent distortion/PAM-calibration or exact re-drizzle provenance is not closed by this ten-file bundle. Exact future measurement procedures must declare the area and weighting conventions before reading pixels.

All ten products identify target `DES16E1DCX`, WFC3/IR F160W, and 2026 CALWF3 `3.7.3`. The first-visit four FLTs belong to `ICXOI1010`, MJD 57715.623–57715.678, and the template four to `ICXOI4010`, MJD 58077.611–58077.637. Current public drizzling at 0.12825 arcsec/pixel and 2026 calibration do not establish equivalence to the historical private RAISIN subtraction, reported at 0.11 arcsec/pixel. `NLINCORR=COMPLETE` records detector accumulated-count correction, not WFC3 IR count-rate nonlinearity.

No image data array was accessed or displayed. No pixel statistic, aperture flux, noise result, SN magnitude or scientific comparison was computed.
