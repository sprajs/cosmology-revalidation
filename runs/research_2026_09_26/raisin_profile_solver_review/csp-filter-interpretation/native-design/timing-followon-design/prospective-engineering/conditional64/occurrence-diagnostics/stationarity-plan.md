# Independent amplitude and peak check: feasibility and next gate

Existing data suffice for an exact **amplitude** profile at each saved peak, but not for an independent two-sided **peak** profile. The saved iteration histories have exact native means at their recorded coordinates; they do not form a prespecified fine grid around the two warned final peaks. The all-call support summary stores domains/counts, not every trial mean vector. Do not substitute the SNooPy rest-grid kernel, a monochromatic calculation, a moving-C optimizer output, or a rounded peak as an exact original-state mean.

For a native mean vector h(t) evaluated at a fixed reference Dref and the saved final observation W=C^-1, define q(t)=h'Wh, b(t)=h'Wy and a*(t)=max(0,b/q). For the positive interior branch, D*(t)=Dref−2.5log10(a*). The independently computed conditional objective is (y−a*h)'W(y−a*h)+((t−tprior)/5)^2, with the **saved final prior center**, fixed nuisance parameters and fixed accepted117 rows. Current final sigma penalties are exactly0. No logdet term varies when W is held fixed; this is a local check of that frozen native objective, not an alternative parameter-dependent-C likelihood or a replacement iterative estimator.

The fixed-point check needs both an amplitude minimum and a two-sided peak local minimum. Direct objective values should be primary; finite-difference gradients alone are insufficient near grid knots or cancellation. A proposed bounded mean grid is t0 plus ±{.25,.125,.0625,.03125,.015625,.0078125,.00390625} observer days, with all native actual R8 coordinates retained. This grid is selected by numerical scale before its values, and is not a truth-centered prior. For any apparent lower neighbor, refine only the corresponding bracket by a predeclared fixed subdivision; report all points and do not change the primary estimator. Compare positive-amplitude minima with t0, track one-sided slopes across scale, second differences and profile-induced D/peak shifts against the existing .001mag/.01day stability scales. Resolution below the source's effective phase precision must be labeled unresolved rather than passed. Source phase/knot nonsmoothness precludes assuming a Hessian exists.

The clean forward route is exact native full observer-frame mean evaluation at these coordinates, preserving K-corrections/warping/MW conventions and the fullR8 fitted t0. A **separate read-only native evaluator/probe process**, with zero-offset mean closure against all117 saved native values, would be required before using these probes. Normal NML peak input is R4 and cannot silently represent the R8 center; separate fixed-peak CLI fits may still provide a declared R4-grid diagnostic, but they do not satisfy that exact center gate. Existing modern-version oracles are not interchangeable with this v11_04d source.

If not, it remains inconclusive and the deliberate mean grid is still needed. This instrumentation would require its own isolated source/build freeze and absent/disabled full-state identity gate; none is implemented or run by this proposal.

Recommended order: complete only the already-prescribed all64 joint9/actual±2 diagnostics under the additive checker, preserve MINUIT warnings, and review whether warned point estimates agree.
