# Timing follow-on: preregistration proposal, not execution authorization

This proposal uses the recovered November2021 source pair and the frozen first-eight precision design. It was written without reading new precision-fit or paired-timing outcomes. The passing historical baseline and numerical state checks are already known. No native execution, new observed fit or distance residual score is performed here. ## Scientific decision

The next identifiable quantity is a **same-photometry, peak-only conditional distance response**. It is not the difference between archived joint and NIR distances: the former also fits shape/extinction and uses optical measurements. Only the archived joint **peak** may be substituted into an otherwise unchanged NIR fit. The resulting peak carries the joint fitter's shape/dust assumptions, near-truth initializer, recentered prior and shared NIR noise; holding the downstream nuisance parameters fixed does not make that upstream peak model independent.

The current precision gate is binding. If any prescribed endpoint/corner/source-time test fails, stop the archived paired intervention for the whole first-eight cohort. Do not discard the failing object, relax a chi-square cap, replace rounded timestamps with a favorable source candidate, or declare a successful mean over survivors. A failed gate diagnoses inadequate precision/control for the stated replay claim; it is not evidence of a timing bias. Even if a common rounding perturbation might cancel in a paired difference, exploiting that cancellation would require a new separately justified proposal, not a retroactive pass of the present gate.

The source amendment is essential: original native epoch MJD was R8, but archived LCPLOT passed through **full-MJD float32 with MJDOFF=0**, then `%.3f`. Its compatible original-time cell has width0.00390625day for these32 epochs, not simply±0.0005day. Flux/error and relevant header coordinates must use their own compatible native float32 states. The candidate SIMLIB timestamps lie inside those cells but do not recover lost flux precision, flags or rejected epochs. These rules and the unchanged0.001mag / max(0.01,0.001×archived dataQ) precision caps are copied by reference from the frozen rounding protocol, never redefined here.

## If precision fails: separate prospective conditional recovery

The existing source SIMLIB plus verified native B18/KCOR assets can support a **new controlled simulation**, not lossless historical recovery. The smallest useful fallback is the separately proposed8 engineering draws followed by64 fresh draws at metadata-selected SIMLIB11 (lowest LIBID with bothJ/H): source z=.453, peak57707.8, MWEBV=.006; fixed true stretch1, AV0 and RV1.518. Preserve all735 source cadence records as input provenance. Full offseason epochs extend well outside the empirical grid, so do not silently generate them with unsupported extrapolation. A first conditional mechanism run may use a prospectively fixed supported-phase cadence, explicitly distinct from full survey generation/selection.

For that controlled run, define the retained source epochs by true rest phase[−15,+45] before drawing noise, and verify native mean support throughout every permitted trial peak. The primary NIR mask is fixed by this metadata, not by the noisy signal or fitted peak. If native peak-relative cuts would remove any such row, the fixed-mask native evaluator must be independently validated; no after-the-fact intersection is permitted. A peak search restricted to a declared finite engineering domain is a conditional diagnostic, not automatically the historical peak-fitting procedure. Boundary solutions and any need to enlarge the domain fail that pilot and require a new proposal.

Generate full-precision optical+NIR measurements once per draw, retain signs, quoted errors, covariance/noise components, seed/substream, truth and every attempted fit. Start with the source photon/background law and explicitly disabled intrinsic scatter; introduce paired OIR.J19 scatter only in a later declared arm. Do not add timing RMS to a scatter model that may already encode timing-related variation.

The minimum paired comparison is: NIR amplitude at known true peak versus NIR amplitude at a peak estimated from the **same generated optical+NIR event**, with the peak-estimation and NIR nuisance shape/dust fixed to the same known truth. The second peak-estimator stage fits peak and amplitude only, removing an avoidable shape/dust optimizer confound from this mechanism test. Its noiseless recovery, actual starts, positive-amplitude scaling, bounded peak-profile check, covariance state and support must pass before64 fresh scored draws. It is deliberately different from the historical free-shape/free-AV joint fit. That historical estimator must later undergo its own reliable profile/multistart validation before a realistic population calculation uses it.

A later optical-only peak estimator on the same optical photons would isolate the contribution of sharing J/H noise. It cannot be reconstructed from the stored joint FITRES and is not presumed to be the creator of observed headers. Never replace the correlated fitted peak by an independent Gaussian draw chosen only to match its marginal SD. Preserve the source near-truth search initializer as one declared condition; a realistic observational discovery/initialization process requires its own identified inputs.

The64-draw paired mean difference and Monte Carlo uncertainty would estimate a conditional expectation under that newly specified fixed truth/cadence/noise law. Record all failures and boundaries, and do not redraw until obtaining64 successes. With a successful fixed-count independent generator, its Monte Carlo SE is sampleSD(δ)/sqrt(64); if fits fail, report failure probability and do not silently renormalize the unconditional target over successful fits. This is useful beyond a flat Gaussian toy because it tests native phase-dependent templates, actual multiband cadence, correlated peak/amplitude noise and the implemented numerical estimator. It does not estimate a survey bias correction.

## What is needed for a selection-normalized correction

For a fully declared generative modelG, measurementY and selected eventS_a under processing arma, a conditional bias target is

b_a(z) = E_G[(D_a(Y)−μ_true)1{S_a(Y)=1}|z] / P_G(S_a(Y)=1|z).

If a prescribed analysis uses redshift-local inverse-variance weights, the numerator and denominator must both include the exact source weight; do not substitute the simple unweighted target. A selection-normalized likelihood likewise divides its selected-data density by its model-dependent selection probability. The native raw mean shift is not that normalization.

To estimate these quantities, generate a fixed, recorded number of attempts from a declared latent population and redshift/cadence sampling law; retain full signed optical+NIR photons, scatter realizations, triggers, classification/follow-up, fit failures and final cuts. Pair all stochastic draws across near-truth and estimated-peak branches. First measure the pure-peak change on a **fixed baseline eligibility set**; then separately report branch-specific selection probabilities and conditional biases. If S changes, do not attribute a change of membership to the pure-peak estimand or use a jointly successful intersection without labelling its conditioning. Numerical failures are not physical selection and need separate counts and recovery gates.

Only the peak is passed into the downstream fixed-nuisance NIR amplitude fit. Realistic nuisance fitting used to *estimate* that peak and real nuisance-based sample cuts remain part of the upstream joint process and must be source-mapped, not varied accidentally across the paired NIR fits. Recompute the entire simulation bias estimate under each declared estimator/selection path. A changed corrected distance is (D1−b1)−(D0−b0), with timing/scatter covariance retained. Templates trained under the previous conventions remain a conditional assumption unless independently retrained or validated.

The archived2021 metadata provide shared truth/initializer fields and coherent accepted NIR rows for500 objects, but no complete generated-attempt ledger, original rejected photometry/flags, full optical photons, effective seed/configuration chain or observational-header creator. SIMLIB supplies observing conditions and candidate times; it does not recreate the missing photon realization or selection denominator. Therefore these assets alone do **not** identify a selection-normalized timing correction or the observed-header bias.

A more limited quantity is possible in principle: after the SIM_DLMAG/scatter definition and archive sampling are source-gated, the mean NIR residual in a specified archived output set is a descriptive conditional fit-residual mean. It neither isolates timing nor represents the unobserved generated/detected population. For the fixed paired500 subset, the difference of empirical mean errors equals meanδ because the common truth cancels; that algebra does not turn it into a population expectation. This proposal performs neither residual calculation. No generic model substitution, new physical dust posterior or corrected cosmology follows.
