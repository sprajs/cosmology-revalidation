# Directional cosmography audit: preregistered plan

Recorded 2026-09-20 before inspecting newly computed likelihoods or synthetic fit outcomes. Parent experiment COS-04. Public code and the existence of its published parameter tables are known in advance. Original source snapshots remain immutable. This is independent distance/light-curve-table work, not a photometry reconstruction.

1. Freeze the live Sah, Rameez and Sarkar arXiv 2606.09650 revision and public repository state. Resolve the README's Zenodo 15629813 covariance record. Audit C1/C2 row identities, sort indices, duplicate observations, matrix shape/symmetry/positive definiteness, covariance versus inverse, error additions, selection cuts, and heliocentric/CMB/HD/LG frame conventions. Inspect scripts without importing them or loading pickle objects.
2. Reimplement a tractable C1 likelihood from equations and public numerical arrays, preserving the supplied covariance and stated intrinsic-scatter/determinant convention. Try isotropic and fixed-direction exponential-dipole fits without and with their public age-correction curve if it can be recovered from safe CSV data or a nonexecuting pickle parser. Preserve public script failures as evidence of reproducibility barriers, not proof of what the authors executed. Compare numerical results to the paper's same-cut/frame/model rows. Exact status requires same inputs, cuts, model and likelihood plus reproduced rounded values; otherwise label approximate and explain.
3. Calculate the cubic luminosity-distance approximation error through z=0.8 for exact flat LambdaCDM Omega_m=0.3 (q0=-0.55, j0=1), Einstein-de Sitter (q0=0.5, j0=1), and flat constant-q=0 coasting (q0=j0=0). Use exact quadrature/closed forms and an independent Astropy LambdaCDM cross-check. Error in distance by itself does not establish bias in fitted q0.
4. Inject each exact model at actual C1 redshifts with the actual C1 covariance weighting. Fit free offset, q0 and j0, with noiseless synthetic vectors first and 100 seeded correlated-noise realizations where feasible (seed 260609650). Evaluate upper cuts z=0.1,0.2,0.4,0.8 with zHEL>0.01; optional same-paper all-positive-redshift sample is separately labelled. Compare q bias to local fitted uncertainty and dispersion; compare to exact-model recovery. Low-z runs test approach to the analytic limit at the cost of precision. A noiseless effect above 0.2 forecast sigma is considered material enough to report, but significance and physical relevance require the full comparison.
5. Test whether an isotropic injection on the real sky can create a spurious fitted exponential q dipole; contrast a cubic self-injection negative control with an exact LambdaCDM injection. Distinguish estimator/model misspecification from evidence for real anisotropy. Avoid converting an uncalibrated profile likelihood ratio into a Gaussian detection significance.

Competing expectations: adequate cubic approximation should recover local q0 within statistical uncertainty and show no substantial redshift-cut dependence; misspecification may shift fitted q0/j0, but the direction and magnitude are not assumed. A nonzero truncation error may be absorbed predominantly by j0/offset, leaving q0 stable. The strongest counterevidence to a truncation criticism is small q0 bias at the real survey weights and stable low-z recovery. The strongest counterevidence to the directional inference is a comparable apparent dipole or frame dependence in controlled isotropic mocks; a mere runtime failure of a public script is insufficient.

Scope limit: C2 requires the full correlated three-variable population likelihood and its exact reordered input. Acquiring a matrix does not alone constitute reproduction. If it cannot be validated/fit within this bounded branch, record precise remaining steps. No external publication or contact.

Decision log: Initial file search inadvertently emitted a short fragment of another investigator's mapping note; no directional inference or test outcome was taken from it. Subsequent source inspection is restricted to this branch until its initial findings are written.
