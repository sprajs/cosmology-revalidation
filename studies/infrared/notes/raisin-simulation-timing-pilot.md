# Approximate archived DES NIR timing pilot: baseline gate failed

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

The [frozen pilot protocol](../specifications/experiments/raisin_profile_solver_review/native-recovery-design/timing-pilot-protocol.json) (SHA-256 `1eed7ec4342929104ee567551b5663db6ef81ef03ce809a38a9cfb02624618e4`) admitted only CIDs 1–8 for a baseline replay of the archived nominal NIR fit. Its baseline gate failed. **No paired optical+NIR peak-time substitution, timing response, simulation bias, or population scoring was run.** The replay is an engineering diagnostic, not a recovered original fit.

**Later archive-lineage correction.** The failed pilot compared a current May 2022 nominal FITRES to a November 2021 LCPLOT. Pinned author history subsequently recovered the [November 2021 NIR and optical+NIR FITRES](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/raisin_timing_assets/historical-tables-acquisition.json) corresponding to that unchanged LCPLOT. The [independent metadata check](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/raisin_timing_assets/historical-coherence-result.json) finds all 500 first-CID row counts, peak clocks, and cadences coherent with the historical NIR FITRES (maximum printed clock gap 0.0005 day), and NIR/joint truth and input fields agree for 29,995 common CIDs. Thus the later-version mismatch explains the failure of the *assumed latest FITRES–older LCPLOT pairing*. It does **not** establish an original HEAD/PHOT input, native baseline closure, or a paired timing effect. The original 8-CID failed gate and all native outputs remain preserved; no replay was rerun against the recovered 2021 table. The pre-correction report bytes are in [correction history](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/correction-history/report-before-20211111-asset-discovery.md).

The first two preserved engineering attempts stopped before fitting. The initial reconstructed `FAKE: 2` header omitted `SIMOPT_MWCOLORLAW`; the native fitter aborted at `INIT_GALextinct`. A source-derived `SIMOPT_MWCOLORLAW: 94` amendment then passed that initialization but aborted because the local model path lacked a recognizable SNooPy name. The third attempt used a recognizable path but resolved an unintended default SNooPy grid (SHA-256 `cc9fc0…`). The final path-only amendment symlinked the released SNooPy B18 grid (SHA-256 `29808a319e7ee5e1ea89ab101c2181e92e2f7752d93e18f8d7b49e10a2400829`) and author-matching `snoopy.info`, with `FITMODEL_NAME='./snoopy.B18'`. The native log (`runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/fits_authorgrid/baseline/fit.log`; historical local reference) confirms the corrected grid and MW law 94. Its [prefit freeze](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/baseline-freeze-authorgrid.json) records code, input, binary, KCOR, and model hashes. The baseline and identical-copy FITRES SN rows and full LCPLOT bytes match exactly.

The [final baseline gate](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/baseline-gate-authorgrid.json) fails the unchanged archived distance tolerance of 0.001 mag for all seven CIDs with replay FITRES. CID 2 has no replay FITRES row. The maximum absolute distance difference is 0.62602 mag (CID 3). CID 4's archived NDOF is 5, versus replay NDOF 1. The printed accepted J/H MJD, flux, and error tokens in the replay LCPLOT match the supplied archived LCPLOT rows for the seven present CIDs, within the prescribed printing/float32 intervals. The corrected author grid did **not** change the replay's fitted FITRES science values from the previous wrong-grid engineering branch; the wrong grid therefore cannot explain away the failed gate. A plot-curve difference is not a substitute for fit-result closure.

An independent [full-500 archive coherence ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/archive-coherence-500.csv), made directly from the two exact Git-pinned compressed author blobs and without using replay outcomes, found **277/500** CIDs whose archived LCPLOT `DATAFLAG=1` row count differs from archived FITRES `NDOF+1`. The nominal NIR fit freezes peak time, shape, AV, and RV, leaving distance as the one fitted parameter. Pinned `snlc_fit.car` packs `NFITDATA + NFITDATA_REJECT` rows into LCPLOT (lines 18240–18268), while NDOF is fit epochs minus fit parameters (lines 4090 and 15365). The archived LCPLOT contains no rejected-data flag rows. Examples: CID 2 has six plotted data rows but FITRES NDOF 2 (three fitted epochs implied); CID 4 has two plotted data rows but FITRES NDOF 5 (six implied). The [machine-readable summary](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/archive-coherence.json) records both blob hashes and all mismatched IDs. Its printed per-row chi-square sums are descriptive only; covariance and print rounding preclude treating that sum as a required global FITCHI2 identity.

An [independent all-500 source audit](raisin-simulation-replay-coherence.md) also checked clock origins and SIMLIB cadences without using the replay fits. Only 28/500 same-CID peak clocks agree within the stated print tolerance, only 42/500 same-CID cadences agree, and only 27 pass both. A candidate remapping was not identifiable: each plotted cadence/clock has 12–1,741 possible FITRES matches among the 30,000 nominal rows, with none unique. This independently supports the CID-indexed closure failure; it does not identify its production mechanism.

Thus the same-directory archived LCPLOT and FITRES do not close as a CID-indexed pair. CID membership and matching version/path labels do not establish row lineage; the audit does not distinguish a writer/indexing issue from files made by separate native executions. Even CID 1 has an LCPLOT time origin (printed MJD minus Tobs approximately 57373.82) matching FITRES CID 2's peak initializer rather than CID 1's 57376.2852, subject to the displayed rounding. The approximately reconstructed `FAKE: 2` text inputs also lack original full-precision epoch photometry, PHOTFLAG, and `SIM_REDSHIFT_HELIO`; the latter was deliberately not invented. These gaps prevent diagnosing every residual replay difference, including CID 1 where the plotted row count agrees. The exact missing asset for a controlled paired timing test is the original simulation HEAD/PHOT used by the archived NIR fit, with fit configuration and an execution-linked FITRES, or a demonstrably coherent full-precision epoch table and FITRES from a single native run. Until then the frozen baseline gate blocks the paired intervention.

All attempted branches, logs, fit tables, input photometry, [runner sources](../code/experiments/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot/baseline_runner_authorgrid.py), and gate checks remain under [`raisin_simulation_timing_pilot`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_simulation_timing_pilot). The path correction was declared and frozen before its fits; it did not change the protocol's outcome thresholds or the eight-ID cap.
