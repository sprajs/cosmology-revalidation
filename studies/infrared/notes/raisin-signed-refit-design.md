# Paired RAISIN optical SNooPy signed-epoch test

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

Status: protocol frozen before new fits; execution requires the source-lineage handoff and generated input manifest. This is a conditional photometry/fitter experiment, not a corrected distance likelihood or a cosmology result.

## Question and source gate

Does restoring actually measured negative optical epochs change the fitted AV, SNooPy stretch, peak date or pre-bias-correction distance modulus under one fixed native observation model? The initial cohort is the ten DES16 aliases with author raw force-photometry ancestors. The seven DES15 aliases have substantive reduction differences and are outside this main experiment. Membership is defined by provenance before any new fit outcome, not by the size or sign of a parameter shift.

Sol's `raisin_flux_sign_audit` supplies the source joins and hashes. Its author-source audit finds 5,378 optical DES16 rows, including 2,243 negatives absent from the positive product and 3,135 positives present. Positive flux values close to their printed precision, but error columns do not close at .001: among unique positive matches the median absolute error difference is .0017 and the maximum .134 flux units. Therefore a release-plus-author-negative file is a labelled hybrid-error counterfactual, not an exact recovery of a historical reduction. No arbitrary scale or sign may be estimated from a preferred SN model. The authoritative negative values are the pinned author raw rows; later DIFFIMG matches corroborate provenance but do not replace those rows.

Gate every input: physical alias and sky identity; author Git-blob integrity; released positive-file byte identity; finite flux and strictly positive quoted errors; matching band/MJD; unchanged redshift, MW reddening, calibration and peak metadata; complete row multiplicity ledger including ambiguities. Preserve raw PHOTFLAG/row/image provenance in a sidecar. Do not add a new quality mask using flags absent from the released positive file. The native accepted-epoch ledger must nevertheless expose any automatic reader/model rejection. Retain all candidates, then apply the same native phase/filter logic in each arm. Do not silently select only negatives that move the answer or only successful objects.

## Four frozen data arms and two timing arms

For each eligible DES16 object, use the released header metadata and optical griz model in four arms:

1. **R — released:** byte-identical released photometry, including its unchanged NIR rows; the optical fitter ignores JH.
2. **A — author positive:** replace optical rows with the author's positive optical rows, preserving their own MJD, flux and error values and multiplicity. Retain released nonoptical rows. This is the explicit processing/rounding bridge.
3. **B — author signed:** exactly A plus all negative (and any zero) author optical rows. A-to-B isolates the removal of signed epochs within the same measured error convention. No sign is guessed or fitted.
4. **H — hybrid restoration:** exactly R plus author negative/zero rows that have no ambiguous overlapping released row. R-to-H is the direct released-positive counterfactual, explicitly conditional on the error-convention bridge. Stop an object's hybrid arm if row identity is ambiguous; retain its failure in the cohort ledger rather than assigning a match by flux.

The primary timing arm follows the released optical NML: peak floats, its initial value comes from the released PEAKMJD, and PRIOR_MJDSIG=5 days is retained. OPT_SETPKMJD=0 explicitly prevents re-estimating that initial peak from the changed row set. The released README says PEAKMJD came from optical+NIR SNooPy fits, so this is an inherited fitted timing constraint, not an independent measurement. The declared sensitivity fixes peak at the same header value with INISTP_PEAKMJD=0. In both, the rest-phase rejection window remains [-7,45] days and native accepted masks are saved; a moving fitted peak can move the free-peak mask. The fixed-peak comparison helps distinguish this pathway. Do not copy a restored-arm fitted peak back into the original arm.

## Exact local assets and native mapping

The release `sources/repos/djones1040__RAISIN_DataRelease@a383c4b` contains `model/snoopy.B18/{snoopy.info,SNooPy_B18.fits}`, `kcor/kcor_DES_NIR.{fits,input}`, `vpec/vpec_baseline_raisin.list` and `lcfitting/REFAC_DES_RAISIN_optical_sys.nml`. The model is an empirical SNooPy EBV_model2 grid, labelled SNooPy_v2.5.2 in the FITS header, with rest filters BVugriYJH, stretch .7–1.3 and phase -20–70 days. The native reader recomputes actual stretch spacing from the 131 tabulated values (about .00461538); the .01 SNPAR-INFO BINSIZE entry is not used as the spacing. Source: `sntools_modelgrid_read.c` lines 249–277 and `genmag_snoopy.c`. Do not call numerical extrapolation beyond these grid limits validated model support; retain and flag fitted boundary/extrapolation cases.

Use the existing `phase2/official/build/SNANA-current/bin/snlc_fit.exe`, hashed together with relevant source files and shared-library resolution. It is a modern controlled comparison, not the historical RAISIN executable. Use an absolute FITMODEL_NAME pointing to the released snoopy.B18 directory and the released KCOR file without rebuilding calibration. Its KCOR table includes observer DES and rest CSP bands and the Hsiao-based K-correction operator. Keep the native SNANA extinction/K-correction behavior; do not substitute BayeSN filters or sncosmo integration.

Starting from the actual released NML, change only local data/model/KCOR/vpec/output paths and declared output diagnostics. Preserve NFIT_ITERATION=3, INTERP_OPT=1, FILTLIST_FIT=griz, OPT_PRIOR_AV=0, INIVAL_SHAPE=1, INIVAL_AV=0, host RV=1.518 with native default step zero, MWEBV_SCALE=1, MWEBV_SHIFT=0, OPT_MWEBV=1, USE_MWCOR=F, OPT_XTMW_ERR=1, OPT_COVAR_FLUX=0 and first-iteration fractional error setting .02. Make native MW law 99 and RV_MWCOLORLAW=3.1 explicit to freeze the modern default; this is not a claim that historical approximate/exact F99 execution is reproduced. No new SNR or chi-square clipping is imposed. The NML's EPCUT_SNRMIN is empty and the native default DELCHI2_REJECT=1e9. AV may be negative under this empirical fit; do not clamp it or interpret it automatically as physical passive dust.

OPT_COVAR_FLUX=0 does not establish a pure quoted-error likelihood: model/K-correction error defaults and Galactic-extinction terms must be inspected in the native log and recorded. Do not import SALT covariance, append the released cosmology systematic covariance, renormalize errors to force chi-square per degree of freedom to one, or add an unvalidated DIFFIMG template-covariance model. Retain native error/covariance failure details. The missing full extraction covariance limits physical uncertainty claims.

## Execution and review gates

Write a manifest containing source hashes, exact cohort, arm row ledgers, NMLs, binary/source/library hashes, environment and command before launch. Native invocation is `snlc_fit.exe /absolute/path/fit.nml`, in an arm-owned output directory, with SNANA_DIR set to the same build, SNDATA_ROOT to the existing official inputs, LD_LIBRARY_PATH to the local sysroot, and OMP/BLAS thread counts one. No global package installation or original-source modification is needed. Run one lexicographically first cohort object's released arm initially, then a zero-change reconstructed copy. Estimate the full runtime from that actual gate; initial expectation is minutes for this ten-object experiment, not a measured runtime guarantee.

Require a successful native initialization of the actual SNooPy grid, KCOR filters and redshift/MW metadata. Confirm the fit's named free/fixed parameters, initial/prior peak, accepted epochs and retained negative fluxes. The positive baseline must close to a byte-identical copied-input rerun: absolute parameter changes <=1e-5 mag in DLMAG/AV, <=1e-5 stretch and <=1e-4 day in peak, with objective difference <=1e-6 relative. Preserve any failed first attempt and explain infrastructure-only repairs before retrying. The source-author A-to-B input check must recover A exactly after removal of its appended nonpositive rows (including its original order/precision). R-to-H must preserve every original positive/nonoptical observation string and metadata field, apart from the mechanically updated NOBS count.

Compare the modern R fit with pinned author raw optical FITOPT000 if obtainable; otherwise use only explicitly reconstructed pre-bias-correction release quantities and mark that limitation. Save the comparison even if it fails. This is a historical-reproduction diagnostic, not a license to tune the new result to match. The within-modern zero-change closure is a separate mandatory gate. A failed historical match permits a labelled modern conditional sensitivity but not a historical-pipeline correction claim.

For the full cohort, retain every failed fit, covariance/Minuit warning, model-support boundary, changed accepted mask and dropped object. Save AV, stretch, peak, DLMAG, their reported covariance/errors, objective and degrees of freedom plus the per-epoch model/data/error/acceptance table. Use deterministic second starts on every eligible fitted object if initialization dependence or a boundary is detected; do not select the preferred start by its parameter-shift direction. Report unconverged or competing minima separately. A small internal reported error is not a substitute for fit closure.

## Estimands and interpretation

Report signed per-object B-minus-A and H-minus-R changes in AV, stretch, peak and pre-BBC DLMAG, with R-minus-A bridge differences alongside them. Summarize medians/ranges and object counts, retain the fixed-peak sensitivity and identify row/mask pathways. No p-value or cosmological significance is predeclared. The pairs share measurements and are not independent; quadrature-adding their reported errors is not an uncertainty on the difference. Different row counts also make a raw chi-square change unsuitable as a model-evidence score.

Root's separate SIMLIB audit finds that observed positive-only cadence was carried into the simulation library. A cadence list derived from positive observations does not itself impose positive selection on newly generated noisy flux at those dates. Without a source-executed matched sign-processing rule and regenerated selection/bias validation, the published correction cannot be presumed to absorb this effect. Conversely, these paired fits alone do not establish the residual cosmology bias. They leave the SN population, object selection, calibration training, extraction covariance, BBC/mass corrections and cosmology fixed or unmodeled. The supported result is a conditional fitted-distance sensitivity to an observable missing-epoch mechanism.
