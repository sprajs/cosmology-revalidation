## Native and arithmetic gates

The original generated-attempt/written/archived-fit/basic-quality counts are P21 26,518/2,678/1,825/1,314 and G10 26,518/3,085/2,164/1,631. Generated-attempt index is the denominator; failed attempts can reuse CID. The frozen 256/arm sample was drawn from archived measured fits across first-PHOT field and six fixed redshift bins, without a quality or truth cut. Eight fixed engineering objects per arm passed fit/export/epoch/covariance gates under each law. Four full native free-parameter fits retained the original quality and clipping settings, with measured archived initialization and no truth peak coordinate. Each arm/law returned 255/256 FITRES objects (99.61%, above the prespecified 95% gate). P21 CID 17254 and G10 CID 13342 have objective lines but no accepted FITRES row and remain failures; no replacement CIDs were chosen.

For the scored 255/arm/law, original PHOT MJD, FLUXCAL and FLUXCALERR match the exported accepted native epochs exactly; the displayed LCPLOT MJD differs by at most 0.0019 day from rounding. Initial measured peak MJD matches the archived seed exactly. Native objective closure is at most `4.69e-13`; all covariance matrices are positive definite (smallest eigenvalue across full runs `1.24589`), and every nuisance tangent has rank four. The accepted scored epochs are P21 9,966 under each law, G10 9,713 under `-99` and 9,712 under `99`; the G10 one-epoch law difference is retained. Parameterized native exports supply the fitted mean, residual, covariance and accepted mask. The amplitude tangent is exact native `-0.4 ln(10) f`; shape/colour/time use the independent local model tangent. Its internal half-step check is at most `4.34e-6`, which is not a native-derivative proof. The independent separate [native finite differences](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/simulation_native_derivatives/result.json) on 48 selected fixed-coordinate exports found score difference at most 0.001603 and information difference at most 0.000220; that is a selected-case sensitivity, not a global bound.

Before scoring, [four law-specific mean-edge ledgers](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/full256) flagged nonpositive independent means or native–independent mean differences above 0.1 quoted sigma. P21 has two flagged epochs on CID 19612 in either law; G10 has six epochs on CIDs 4058, 7349, 24318 under `-99` and two under `99`. Native zero means and independent negative means are preserved without Python clipping or epoch deletion. The independent [fixed-native derivative edge check](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/sim_edge_J/result.json) finds a large projector difference for P21 19612 (0.799) and G10 24318 (0.432), but their fixed-gain differences are about 0.0117 and at most 0.00060; both fail archived basic quality. G10 4058/7349 pass that quality stage and have fixed-gain differences at most 0.00051. These are implementation sensitivities on the four flagged CIDs, not an all-object derivative bound. The primary score keeps every native accepted row.

The [runner](../code/simulation_residual_control.py) and [score manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/score/manifest.json) hash the executable/configuration/source and all input/output tables. An [independent verifier](../code/experiments/simulation_residual_control/verify_score.py) rehashed 52 listed files and checked 1,020 per-object sufficient-score rows, every transport and field sum, and 32,000 bootstrap records; the largest `a`, `I`, `G` arithmetic discrepancy was `6.66e-16`. ## Fixed-direction outcomes

Each object has matched product `a`, information `I`, and fixed gain `G=a-I/2` after projecting out four fitted SALT directions. The coefficient vector is the unchanged mean from the exact 43 DES discovery objects. The simulation transport weight in each supported first-PHOT field/redshift cell is `n_real_cell/n_sim_cell`; real comparator objects receive equal weight within the retained cells. Cells require at least two successful objects in **both** arms. The primary table uses the generation-consistent `-99` law and field/redshift transport; [all raw and weighted rows](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/score/transport-summary.csv), [object scores](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/score/object-scores.csv), [field contributions](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/score/field-contributions.csv) and [bootstrap draws](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_residual_control/score/bootstrap.csv.gz) are saved.

The common stage supports 52 cells and all 1,020 real objects; archived-quality supports 42 cells and 888 real objects. The newly computed basic-quality stage has the same selected counts in this pilot, but is retained separately in the result table. The `99` implementation control yields common-stage amplitudes P21 +0.0542 and G10 -0.0147, with the same real comparator; archived quality yields +0.0779 and -0.0513. Adding the prespecified SNRMAX1 split reduces common-stage real support to 850/1,020 and yields amplitudes +0.0235/-0.0566 versus real +0.5796. This uses archived simulated and measured real FITRES SNRMAX1, without claiming identical earlier epoch acceptance. The archived-quality SNR split retains 734/1,020 real objects and amplitudes +0.0386/-0.1314 versus real +0.6034.

P21 has 14/256 members with negative attenuation somewhere on the declared 2800–8000 Å optical grid; the source-verified generator uses its host screen. The secondary **nonnegative on declared grid** subset retains 241/255 successful P21 objects (one of the 242 frozen grid-nonnegative CIDs failed FITRES), of which 219 fall in supported cells. Its transported amplitude is +0.0323 under `-99`, versus +0.5824 in the same 1,020 real objects. This label is a necessary grid check, not physical validation across all model wavelengths. G10's AV/RV `-9` entries are source-confirmed no-explicit-host-screen sentinels.

The 1,000 fixed-support LIBID-block resamples have zero empty-information replicates. Descriptive 2.5–97.5% simulation-amplitude ranges are P21 `[-0.432,+0.493]` and G10 `[-0.477,+0.440]` at common success; at archived quality they are `[-0.421,+0.557]` and `[-0.521,+0.425]`. They express finite cadence-block variation in this one DES-overlapping generated realization; they are **not** calibrated coverage for the real survey. The simulated fixed direction is smaller than in real objects under these declared stage/support definitions. Neither arm supplies the missing matched-classifier selection, independent retrained population, shared-calibration uncertainty, or repeated-realization validation needed to assign a physical cause. The published classifier is itself only partially reproducible; its [documented gap](classifier.md) remains in force.
