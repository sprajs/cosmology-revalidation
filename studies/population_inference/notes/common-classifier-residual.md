# Common reconstructed-classifier sensitivity in P21/G10 pilots

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This is the separately frozen, post-primary [selection sensitivity](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/common-classifier-protocol.md) (SHA-256 `e060491bc41b217f0c78065e08ffc60009b7e12b914a617c197bbdc663a358b8`). It applies one executable, pinned SuperNNova V19 measured-input classifier to the existing 256 P21 and 256 G10 simulation pilots, then selects their **unchanged** native epoch-residual scores. It does not reopen the failed released `PROB_SNNV19` reproduction gate, add another simulation arm, refit the exact-43 observer vector, or interpret simulation truth Ia as a classifier cut. The [primary unselected residual control](simulation-residual-control.md) and its outputs remain unchanged.

The original real-clump binary SHA-256 `785bfaf84727d121332c18e86babcf4d881daa31ce944719f784de276902b3b4` was rerun separately on the existing written P21/G10 HEAD/PHOT files. Its NML changed only `PRIVATE_DATA_PATH`, `VERSION_PHOTOMETRY`, and `TEXTFILE_PREFIX` from the preserved real clump. Thus the measured alignment remains `OPT_SETPKMJD=16` with `PHOTFLAG_MSKREJ=1016`; the archived simulation fit's option 20 peak was not used as a classifier coordinate. Every frozen CID has a finite clump peak and valid inclusive HEAD/PHOT pointers. The five common-minus-archived peak differences are P21 CID 3413 (-3.9883 days) and G10 CIDs 11278 (-7.0156), 13160 (+8.0820), 13349 (+15.9727), 20240 (-7.8164); all other 507 pilot peaks agree. This [peak ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/common_classifier_residual/inference/peak-ledger.csv) is provenance, not a basis for choosing the better score.

The official onthefly wrapper, pinned model and normalization use strict observed-frame `-30 < MJD-PKMJDINI < 100` **before** the seven PHOTFLAG-bit rejections, then the official 0.33-day grouping. The 512 pilot light curves have 44,199 raw rows, 11,346 window exclusions, zero subsequent flagged exclusions, and 32,853 retained rows. Features use recorded `REDSHIFT_FINAL[_ERR]`; no truth peak, redshift, AV, RV or type enters inference. A fixed five CIDs per arm inferred singly and in 128-object batches agree exactly, with no threshold-membership change or probability within `1e-6` of 0.999. The 1,020 real validation objects rerun with the same preserved real clump and wrapper agree with their prior corrected diagnostic to `1.11e-16` maximum and reproduce the identical 1,006-member intersection. P21 has 152/256 strict reconstructed `pIa > .999`; G10 has 151/256. The [inference outputs](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/common_classifier_residual/inference) include every probability, preprocessing count, and gate.

The primary comparison applies the single probability per arm/CID to both native-law scores and requires **archived basic quality AND reconstructed** `pIa > .999`. The real target is only the 1,006 original-validation objects passing both published and reconstructed thresholds; no reconstructed-only real object enters. For each arm/stage/law, field/redshift cells require at least two objects in both arms, with weights `n_real_cell/n_sim_cell`. The score table records each stage's available support and, separately, both pre/post quality stages on the **same post-classifier cells**. Real weights are uniform among retained real objects. This executable common-classifier sensitivity still differs from the unavailable original Pippin classifier selection. Its 582-object supported real comparison also loses 424 of the 1,006 intersection objects, so it cannot adjudicate the full original high-Ia residual. These truth-Ia pilot arms, DES-overlapping trained components, finite cadence realization, and approximate non-amplitude native tangent remain additional limits. The result narrows one measured-input selection discrepancy without identifying the source of the real observer-colour residual.
