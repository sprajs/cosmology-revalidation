Required before strong ranking or coverage interpretation:

- The projected variance `[1,-f_x,-f_c] V [1,-f_x,-f_c]^T` is a residual-error propagation formula. It is not generally the exact conditional variance of mB given noisy observed x1,c under a finite predictor population. The quadratic and zero-colour hinge introduce additional nonlinear mean and variance errors. The stated descriptive interpretation is essential; coefficient values and model wins must not be relabeled physical corrections.
- A ±2 predictive-standard-deviation fraction is not 95% posterior predictive coverage for host mixtures or Student tails. Report posterior-integrated predictive CDF/PIT and actual interval quantiles, alongside the clearly labeled moment envelope.
- Comparisons need paired per-object log-score differences on fixed IDs, posterior Monte Carlo uncertainty of the score integration, object-bootstrap uncertainty, and ten-field cluster / leave-one-field sensitivity. R-hat and effective sample size of parameters do not alone certify small Monte Carlo error in tail predictive densities. A winner among 18 candidates has model-selection uncertainty.
- Declare the single multimodal-lightcurve sensitivity and covariance perturbation sensitivity before scores are opened. Coherent local covariance does not integrate over a second light-curve mode; small marginal error differences do not bound errors in the projected correction direction.
- The finite knot curve and Gaussian offset priors do not exactly absorb an arbitrary distance reference. The reference is common across candidates, but its regularization remains informative. A “free redshift curve” claim needs a reference/regularization sensitivity, and no age-versus-cosmology distinction follows from such a curve.
- The high-SNN threshold conditions on a classifier using overlapping flux. This can define a selected-cohort prediction problem; it is not independent purity evidence or the parent selection probability. Host mixing also conditions on Gaussian mass errors and a locally flat host-mass prior, with no mass–SALT measurement covariance supplied.
- Applying a Student4 distribution to total projected variance is a valid descriptive density. It is not the same physical model as Student intrinsic scatter convolved with Gaussian measurement errors. Do not attribute a tail-family score improvement uniquely to intrinsic astrophysical scatter.

[Independent analytical counterexamples](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/inference_audit/independent_counterexamples.json) demonstrate the distinction between a normalized empirical density and a physical latent-predictor likelihood:

- For latent stretch SD1, stretch measurement SD0.5, true slope0.3 and independent magnitude error0.05 plus scatter0.1, the exact conditional slope given observed stretch is0.24. Exact conditional variance is0.0305, versus0.035 from residual propagation at the physical slope. Their expected score difference is0.0688 nats/object in this deliberately specified Gaussian example. It is not a measured DES score bias.
- With latent colour SD0.08, colour measurement SD0.05 and physical mean `3c + 2 max(c,0)`, conditioning on observed colour zero gives mean offset+0.03383mag, whereas the plug-in hinge gives zero. Exact predictive variance is0.04032 versus0.03340 projected variance, including the stated magnitude error/scatter. At observed colours−0.05/+0.05 the plug-in mean errors are−0.0515/+0.0609mag. These are genuine Jensen/shrinkage effects, not normalization errors.
- Quadratic stretch has a nonzero latent variance contribution and mean shift even at observed stretch zero, where the local derivative vanishes. The saved exact Gaussian moments quantify this counterexample.
- Convolving Student4 intrinsic scatter of SD0.1 with Gaussian measurement SD0.2 gives68.95% probability within one total SD; treating the total error as Student4 gives76.98%. Both densities are valid predictive models, but they represent different physical noise assumptions.
- Host mixing is correctly implemented as a density mixture. A fractional host substitution would generally give a different shape and variance. The remaining uncertainty is the host-weight model, not an identified mixture algebra error.

## Minimal numerical repair, explicitly authorized

The original exponential–Gaussian density formula added `x²/2` to `log Phi(x)`. At dust scale1e−8, cancelling two large terms caused a maximum log-density error0.007814 on the registered stress grid. That scale lies far outside practical mass under the stated dust prior; this was a numerical robustness issue, not evidence of a scientific population result changing.

With the root's authorization, only `scripts/phase2/hierarchy/core.py` was edited. A helper evaluates `log(exp(x²/2) Phi(x))` using the alternating Mills series through x⁻¹² for x≤−20. The next omitted term bounds the relative truncation error by8.3e−14 at the switch. Safe inactive arguments avoid invalid autodifferentiation branches. The exGaussian CDF second term was algebraically rewritten to use the same helper, avoiding cancellation without changing the model. The switch includes exactly−20 because the installed JAX `log_ndtr` branch has a small pre-existing value/gradient error there.

[Independent validation](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/inference_audit/stable_tail_validation.json) uses SciPy `erfcx`, rescaled numerical integrals and derivative integrals. Maximum density-grid error after the fix is4.97e−14, scaled-tail value error7.99e−14, and tiny-scale CDF error1.42e−14. The τ→0 Gaussian limit and finite gradients pass. The existing root quadrature/selection/distance suite also passes: convolution error≈1.19e−14 and selection log-normalization error≈5e−16. Original source and pre-change validation outputs are preserved under [pre_mills](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/inference_audit/pre_mills/preserved_manifest.json). Initial direct quadrature missed a very narrow boundary peak in one stress case; rescaling the integration variable corrected that reference failure before drawing conclusions or changing root code.

This numerical patch does not affect the distance-only calls used by the running conditional benchmark. It does not repair the separate scientific approximation in `v V v`.

## Selection-normalization boundary and next work

The forward-discrimination agent now owns the nine-arm selected-observable and selection-response comparisons. I coordinated a narrower mathematical boundary:26,518 retained generated attempts in each arm, with attempt index rather than reused CID as the denominator key, identify acceptance probabilities for the **specific simulated selector and population**. They do not by themselves identify the historical BBC/systematic-intersection/high-SNN selector or an arbitrary new parent law.

A precise obstruction is the unobserved final selector. Let A be the simulated trigger/host-z/basic-fit pass and B the historical classifier/BBC membership decision. The simulations identify `P(A|theta)`, but actual normalization requires `P(A and B|theta)`. Many colour/host-dependent functions `P(B|A,y)` can produce the same available A outputs, and even the same overall count, while changing the selected shape and relative model normalization. Counts cannot identify that missing function. Conditioning on measured error or fit probability also requires their joint distribution with flux and selection; treating these random fit outcomes as externally fixed does not supply the missing population likelihood.

The released source luminosity law is singular conditional on its full latent variables. Importance ratios for arbitrary shifted luminosity/cosmology laws need an absolute-continuity/support proof or fresh injections. The existing finite luminosity-shift arms provide useful discrete response checks, not a general continuous normalization. Unknown host/cadence proposal conditioning and the truth-Ia versus classifier-selected-cohort difference remain material. A defensible immediate forward estimand is retrospective conditional predictive checking on common support, with train-only cell/offset construction, finite-simulation uncertainty, paired attempt bootstraps and explicit unsupported held-out counts. It cannot declare a unique physical dust law from selected-shape agreement.

No real parent-population fit, real conditional winner, or physical correction ranking was opened or certified by this audit. The reported Tripp computational failure independently blocks an empirical ranking at this pause. Bayesian SBC, broad prior/multimodality sensitivity, empirical selector validation and independent flux compression beyond the pilot remain unresolved.
