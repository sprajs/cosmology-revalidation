# Disjoint same-catalogue P21/G10 residual holdout

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

The [frozen extension protocol](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/simulation-holdout-protocol.md), SHA-256 `8f5cd945582f84486c380fc1d406c53fc4305f1278177fd30a8fa23b98d8a2a1`, was written after the 256-per-arm pilot and before any holdout fixed-vector score. The [design manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/astra_design/simulation_holdout_design/manifest.json) freezes **every remaining metadata-eligible archived fit** in the same original simulation catalogues: 1,569 P21 and 1,908 G10 CIDs. All 256 pilot IDs per arm, including its native failures, are excluded from the holdout. This is additional-object validation within one generated catalogue per arm, **not** an independent simulation seed or calibrated physical-null coverage. No new synthetic light curve, alternative physical population, threshold, coefficient, or fit setting was chosen from residual outcomes.

The holdout used the generation-consistent current `OPT_MWCOLORLAW=-99` free-parameter SNANA fit only. Its measured seeds came from archived double-precision fitted parameters, never truth peak or truth SALT coordinates. The original ordinary quality/clipping settings remained, with private CID list, output and objective/LCPLOT export added. The two isolated native jobs returned FITRES/objective counts P21 **1,563/1,569** and G10 **1,903/1,908**, or 99.62%/99.74% accepted, above the prospective 95% gate. P21 missing FITRES CIDs are `2548, 7871, 9751, 9971, 11392, 13844`; G10 missing CIDs are `4815, 15565, 16809, 21299, 23781`. All have objective lines but remain failures and contribute no score. Generator inputs, historical binary/source/logs, SIM flux-error map, SIMLIB, KCOR, SALT3 surface, search efficiencies and current fitter source are hashed in the 37-file [generator/noise provenance](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control/generator-noise-provenance.json); exact native fit seeds, NMLs, logs and hashes are under [the run directory](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control).

The original pilot provenance gate failed when applied unchanged to this larger cohort: `MXLC_PLOT=1000` supplied no LCPLOT duplicate for P21 CID 17250 despite 48 native objective accepted epochs. That [failure](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control/baseline-gate-failure.json) is retained. The source-backed [amended gate](../code/experiments/simulation_holdout_control/gate_holdout.py) verifies that `PHASE2_FLUX` is emitted from the fitter's `EPLIST_FIT` accepted rows, then matches **every** exported accepted native MJD/band/FLUXCAL/FLUXCALERR exactly to inclusive raw HEAD/PHOT, and closes fitted coordinates, frozen covariance and objective. LCPLOT agrees wherever available and covers exactly the **first 1,000 successful objects in native objective order**, with no sporadic omission before the cap; the remaining 563 P21 and 903 G10 objects are explicitly unplotted. P21/G10 accepted scored epoch totals are 60,388/73,313; smallest covariance eigenvalues are 1.1208/1.1034; objective closure is at most `3.69e-13`/`2.27e-13`. The [P21](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control/P21/provenance-gate.json) and [G10](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control/G10/provenance-gate.json) gate JSONs record plot coverage, source hashes and all raw-unit checks. No second free fit was run solely to produce a redundant plot table.

All scored objects have rank-four fitted nuisance tangents. The independent tangent half-step maxima are `5.94e-6` P21 and `7.63e-6` G10; these internal checks are **not** global native-derivative bounds. Before scoring, implementation [mean-edge ledgers](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control) flagged 38 accepted P21 epochs on 26 CIDs and 53 G10 epochs on 31 CIDs with nonpositive native/independent means or >0.1 quoted-sigma differences. No epoch was dropped or independent mean clipped. Source-identified four-CID native derivative sensitivity is a separate bounded check; the [selection ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/simulation_holdout_control/native-edge-selection.json) names P21 `19675, 14533` and G10 `980, 6801`. Its selection used only pre-existing implementation ledgers, while object-score computation was underway and before any aggregate result was read. It is not represented as pristine pre-computation registration.

The same pinned SuperNNova model and already closed real/pilot wrapper inferred **all 3,477** holdout photometry objects from the pre-existing simulation clump built with measured `OPT_SETPKMJD=16` and `PHOTFLAG_MSKREJ=1016`, never archived option-20 peaks or simulation truth. The strict observed-frame window precedes flags. Across both arms it retained 225,782 of 307,394 raw epochs after 81,612 window exclusions and zero further flagged exclusions. Five fixed single/batch CIDs per arm agree within `8.20e-8`, with no object within `1e-6` of the `.999` threshold. P21 has 882/1,569 and G10 1,129/1,908 strict reconstructed `pIa>.999`.
