# Synthetic recovery of a one-factor censored amplitude

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

The frozen synthetic recovery screen completed all 128 paired realizations. In this constructed 74-coordinate problem, fitting a Gaussian only to each realization's newly positive measurements overestimates the true amplitude by **1.0006 ± 0.0171** (Monte Carlo standard error). The full signed Gaussian, joint-censored likelihood, and independently archived fixed-mask Gaussian have biases consistent with zero at this Monte Carlo precision. The full-pattern-conditioned estimator also has a mean near the truth but is less precise and reaches the zero boundary on three realizations. These results test estimator behavior under the declared generator; they are not RAISIN data corrections.

The [frozen design protocol](../../infrared/specifications/experiments/raisin_profile_solver_review/onefactor-review/v2/recovery-protocol.json) has SHA-256 `39e07714c9c9de2b61d1a3ec3378335e14e9d3c6822202da1c8543d9fffb4bf0`. Before any estimator outcome, the [executable and complete draws](../specifications/experiments/onefactor_recovery/execution-protocol.json) were frozen (SHA-256 `384be72d3ec0c79bc1633fbba6fa878d25f12902eaf51b4499ee05591b20dba4`). The truth is `h_i = 0.5 sqrt(C_ii)`, amplitude 1, and `y=h+vZ+sqrt(d) ε` with one latent draw plus 74 independent normals. The covariance is a saved B-anchor mathematical design, not validated extraction noise. All five estimators received the same complete new vector on each draw; the fifth used one independent old-noise positive mask fixed across new draws. New positive counts ranged 43–61; the old mask has 51.

The first-eight [timing gate](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/benchmark-gate.json) passed in 3.39 seconds and forecast 54.3 seconds for all 128, below the frozen 600-second single-worker cap. The remaining 120 took 50.2 seconds. All 256 numerical fits passed nested-grid, score-step, Hermite-order and independent adaptive checks. There were **2,205** independent adaptive candidate checks; the largest 64/128/256-node versus adaptive log-likelihood gap was `1.42e−13`, the largest coarse/fine optimum-amplitude gap `1.44e−7` (tolerance `1e−5`), and the largest two-step score gap `2.98e−9` (tolerance `1e−6`). A separate direct-matrix [analytic check](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/analytic-check.json) reproduces all 384 Gaussian estimates and truth scores within `1.43e−14`. No unsupported, optimization-failed, tied or upper-boundary case occurred. The [per-draw stream](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/draw-results.jsonl), [summary](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/summary.json) and [numerical diagnostics](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/postprocess.json) retain every estimate and check.

Paired with the full signed estimate on the **same** 128 noise realizations, the mean amplitude changes are +1.0159 ± 0.0178 (newly positive Gaussian), +0.0004 ± 0.0135 (archived mask), −0.0046 ± 0.0071 (joint censored), and −0.0419 ± 0.0286 (full-pattern conditioned). The truth-score diagnostic is within four MC standard errors for the two proper likelihoods; the naive positive-only score is not centered because its likelihood omits the sign-selection mechanism. Correct likelihoods can still have finite-sample MLE bias.

For `ΔD = −2.5 log10(amplitude)`, reported separately, mean finite values are +0.0511 ± 0.0249, −0.7479 ± 0.0093, +0.0709 ± 0.0321, +0.0608 ± 0.0266, and +0.1900 ± 0.0781 mag in the table's order. The last mean includes only **125/128** finite outcomes; conditional draws 15, 92 and 121 have amplitude exactly zero and undefined log distance. Paired log-distance changes versus full signed are −0.7990 ± 0.0208, +0.0198 ± 0.0176, +0.0097 ± 0.0092 on 128 pairs, and +0.1446 ± 0.0712 on the conditional arm's 125 finite pairs. The nonlinear log transform can have Jensen bias even when amplitude is unbiased. No undefined value was converted to a finite number or silently included in a mean.

The full-pattern likelihood divides the joint censored density by the probability of **all 74 realized signs**. A [direct truth-amplitude check](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/onefactor_recovery/normalizer-check.json) for draw 0 finds full-pattern log probability −49.0952 versus −17.2958 for an A-only positive event; substituting the latter would change the conditional log likelihood by 31.7994. The frozen executable uses `np.where(positive, 1, −1)` over the complete 74-vector. The joint-censor arm has no additional normalizer.

The available factor covariance and synthetic template do not establish an observed RAISIN cadence, dust law, population, nuisance covariance or cosmological result. A native-template recovery experiment would require its own fixed truths and execution gates.
