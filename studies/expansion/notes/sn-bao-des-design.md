# Outcome-free DES-SN5YR versus transverse-BAO design

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

The released DES-SN5YR distance branch supports a **three-node** relative SN–transverse-BAO forecast at BAO effective redshifts **0.510, 0.706 and 0.934**, with one free intercept. The primary conditional standard error for an endpoint drift linear in `ln(1+z)` across 0.510–0.934 is **0.04234 mag**. This is a forecast from released redshifts and covariance only. Neither `MU` nor any observed distance residual was loaded or scored.

The [DES-specific protocol](../specifications/experiments/sn_bao_des_design/protocol.json) was frozen before design computation (SHA-256 `ed47ecab17612d4df126c9e5fb5076453a52871aa614cdf496624334a98a6b2d`), with all source and input hashes. Its [source snapshot](../code/sn_bao_des_design.py) is byte-identical to the executed source. This branch retains the original Pantheon+ design's four interpolation settings, 18 recovery curves, admission gates, free-intercept forecast definition, and 0.02/0.05/0.10 mag endpoint alternatives. No gate was relaxed to admit another node.

## Release mapping and covariance

The release HD table (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/DES-SN5YR_HD.csv`; historical local reference) has 1,829 rows, of which the first **1,635** are DES (`IDSURVEY=10`) in exactly the supplied order; all 1,635 CIDs are unique. These rows and their `zHD`, `zHEL`, and `MUERR_FINAL` columns match the separate metadata table (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/DES-SN5YR_HD+MetaData.csv`; historical local reference) row for row. The DES range is `zHD=0.05962–1.12132` and `zHEL=0.05999–1.12212`. Interpolation is in `ln(1+zHD)`. `zHEL` enters only the *unscored future* SN geometry expression `MU−5log10[(1+zHEL)zHD]`, consistent with the release's distinction between heliocentric and Hubble-diagram redshift.

The release covariance README (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/README.md`; historical local reference) and SN-only likelihood (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/5_COSMOLOGY/SN_only_cosmosis_likelihood.py`; historical local reference) specify the same convention: the `STAT+SYS` file supplies **additional systematic covariance**; add `MUERR_FINAL²` to its diagonal **once**. `STATONLY` is exactly zero and is not added. The full 1,829-row matrix was loaded in release order, then restricted by the 1,635 DES row indices. It is symmetric and the resulting DES covariance is positive definite. The reported forecast includes the released statistical/BEAMS uncertainty and released cross-object systematics; it is conditional on those published quantities. It is not a new independent calibration prior.

The [DESI DR2 Gaussian BAO mean and covariance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CobayaSampler/bao_data/bb0c1c9009dc76d1391300e169e8df38fd1096db/desi_bao_dr2/desi_gaussian_bao_ALL_GCcomb_mean.txt) contribute only `DM_over_rs` nodes inside the DES redshift support, and their **marginal** covariance submatrix is transformed to logarithmic magnitudes. `DV` and `DH` are excluded, and no inverse-full-covariance conditionalization is used. The SN and BAO cross-probe covariance is unavailable and taken as zero for this forecast.

## Frozen support and forecast

All 12 design/node combinations pass the unchanged thresholds: at least 20 distinct CID total, at least five strictly on either side, full polynomial rank, condition number below `1e8`, and at most 0.005 mag recovery bias on the 18 named smooth test curves. The largest finite-family interpolation error among the 12 is `9.55e-5` mag. The 96-versus-192 point quadrature check reaches `6.44e-15` mag. The [full design table](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/sn_bao_des_design/design-table.csv) retains each local window, condition number, weight size and error.

All three nodes are admitted jointly across settings. The primary point standard deviations, after SN and BAO covariance are combined, are 0.02858, 0.02550 and 0.03153 mag. Local SN estimates overlap, so the forecast uses the complete `W C_DES Wᵀ` cross-node covariance rather than independent-node errors. A 50,000-draw BAO delta-method check gave a 0.00880 relative Frobenius difference from the analytic transformed covariance; this is a numerical approximation check, not a physical history test.

An [independent readback](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/sn_bao_des_design/independent-check.json) reconstructed the DES systematic-plus-statistical covariance and row order, recomputed all three primary local GLS weights with whitened QR, and removed the common intercept with an explicit Helmert basis. The maximum weight difference is `3.13e-16`, covariance difference `1.52e-18 mag²`, and it reproduces the primary SE and all three powers. The [machine-readable result](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/sn_bao_des_design/result.json) and weights (`runs/research_2026_09_26/sn_bao_des_design/weights.npz`; historical local reference) preserve the remaining detail and hashes.

This is a separately labelled DES forecast, **not** an independent-dataset multiplication with the Pantheon+ branch: the compilations share supernovae and analysis ancestry. It does not establish an observed drift, opacity, luminosity evolution, a cosmological correction or a validated arbitrary-history BAO likelihood. The released effective-redshift Gaussian BAO compression and broad redshift kernels remain the limiting gate even though the named smooth interpolation curves recover well.
