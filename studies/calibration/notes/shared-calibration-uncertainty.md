# Shared calibration and SALT3 uncertainty in the DES release

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

The exact SNANA light-curve covariance used for one object does **not** include a covariance between different supernovae from a common uncertain photometric calibration or a common uncertain trained SALT3 surface. The original DES distance release **does** supply a separate cross-SN systematic covariance for its tested calibration/SALT3 variations. Thus an observer-band or field-sign pattern scored with fixed nominal calibration, fixed SALT3 and the per-object epoch covariances is a *conditional residual test*. It cannot by itself reject the release's full systematic budget or establish an unmodelled correction. This distinction also applies to the 43-object exact-SNANA control described in [calibration-investigation.md](calibration-investigation.md).

## What the two covariances contain

The contemporaneous pinned SNANA source builds `COVMAT2(irow,icol)` for `irow,icol=1..NFITDATA` of **one** fitted object. It calls `gencovar_SALT2` with that object's fit parameters, observed filter list and epochs, converts its model-mag covariance to flux units, and adds `COV_MWXT` and `COV_DATA` before inverting the matrix (`snlc_fit.car`, `FITINI_COV` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-2fe0f56/src/snlc_fit.car`; historical local reference), around lines 10170–10330). `gencovar_SALT2` returns an `MATSIZE × MATSIZE` magnitude covariance; its off-diagonal color-dispersion term is within the same passband, and its diagonal contains `SALT2magerr` from the model error maps (`genmag_SALT2.c` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-2fe0f56/src/genmag_SALT2.c`; historical local reference), around lines 3185–3305). The later local SNANA source preserves the same per-object structure (`snlc_fit.F90` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-current/src/snlc_fit.F90`; historical local reference), around lines 10590–10720). `COV_MWXT` can correlate epochs *within* an object; this is not a shared Milky Way map or calibration covariance across objects. Fixed SALT3 variance, model-variance/covariance and color-dispersion maps may reflect training uncertainty and empirical scatter locally, but applying those maps independently to every object does not integrate over a common uncertain SALT3 surface shared by all objects.

At the next level, the methods paper defines the supernova-distance covariance as diagonal statistical uncertainty plus a systematic matrix built from weighted outer products of distance shifts across SNe (Vincenzi et al. 2024, §3.6, Eq. 11 (`papers/text/2401.02945v2.txt`; historical local reference), local text around lines 637–666). The original release has 1,829 rows; `MUERR_FINAL` holds the statistical diagonal and `STAT+SYS.txt.gz` the **additional** systematic covariance. `STATONLY.txt.gz` is zero, so the release likelihood adds `diag(MUERR_FINAL²)` once (distance README (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/README.md`; historical local reference), lines 65–86; the released likelihood source (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/5_COSMOLOGY/SN_only_cosmosis_likelihood.py`; historical local reference), lines 68–90, explicitly performs this addition). The `CALIBplusSALT3` single-systematic file represents calibration and light-curve modeling, with a separately listed `CALSPEC` file (component README (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/4_DISTANCES_COVMAT/SingleSYS_CovMatrix/README.md`; historical local reference), lines 1–29). The paper's cosmology likelihood uses the inverse statistical-plus-systematic *distance* covariance (DES cosmology paper (`papers/text/2401.02929v4.txt`; historical local reference), around lines 543–560). In a Gaussian distance likelihood, it reduces weight on coherent patterns from the *tested* calibration/SALT3 and other listed variations, as a covariance-level marginalization over those second moments. It is not a direct signed epoch-flux covariance, an explicit posterior over calibration parameters, or a guarantee over every possible calibration error.

The [47-component release audit](../../dust/notes/salt-dust-audit/correction-matrices.md) finds that the original `CALIBplusSALT3` group already contains the standalone `CALSPEC` direction under the saved Pippin grouping rule. Adding both individual files would double count it; the released total already includes the calibrated group. That audit also documents covariance-format differences in the later Dovekie release and an original unassigned grouping remainder already included in the original total. Neither is an extra correction to apply here.

## What a joint epoch-level comparison still needs

The public release supplies **signed calibration shift lists and alternative full SALT3 surfaces**, so a fixed-parameter forward-model difference for those specific variants is in principle computable. Their exact machine-readable [nine-variant inventory](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/calibration_provenance/des_salt3_calibration_variants.json), [DES four-band table](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/calibration_provenance/des_salt3_calibration_variants.csv) and separate [hash manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/calibration_provenance/des_salt3_calibration_variants_manifest.json) were made by a [read-only script](../code/experiments/calibration_provenance/variant_inventory.py). All nine archived lists agree entry-for-entry with their `SALT3.MODEL001..009/SALT3.INFO` embeddings (203–205 shifts each), and `SUBMIT.INFO` (`sources/repos/des-science__DES-SN5YR@1.3/2_LCFIT_MODEL/SALT3.DES5YR-SYS/SUBMIT.INFO`; historical local reference) maps `TRAINOPT001..009` to the corresponding list filenames. The inventory hashes each list, model file, source/configuration and upstream covariance copy.

The pinned SNANA call path makes the coupling precise. `FITMODEL_NAME` selects the variant directory; `INIT_GENMAG_SALT2` reads that model's `SALT3.INFO` `MAGSHIFT`/`WAVESHIFT` records (`genmag_SALT2.c` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-2fe0f56/src/genmag_SALT2.c`; historical local reference), around lines 1288–1316), and `init_calib_shift_SALT2train` matches the survey and full filter base to the KCOR-loaded filters, adding a positive `MAGSHIFT` to `magprimary` and a positive `WAVESHIFT` in Angstrom to the filter wavelength grid (same source, around lines 1938–2027 and 2050–2140; `genmag_SEDtools.c` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-2fe0f56/src/genmag_SEDtools.c`; historical local reference), around lines 220–345). The historical fitter defaults both `ENABLE_MAGSHIFT_SALT2` and `ENABLE_WAVESHIFT_SALT2` to true (`snlc_fit.car` (`phase2/official/build/SNANA-2fe0f56/src/snlc_fit.car`; historical local reference), around lines 7168–7169), and no disabling override is in the saved DES fit input. Thus selecting a variant is designed to apply the direct calibration perturbation **and** its retrained surface, conditional on matching loaded filters; using a changed surface alone would omit the direct leg. The original DES nominal KCOR input (`phase2/official/inputs/SNDATA_ROOT/kcor/DES/DES-SN5YR/calib_DES-SN5YR_DES.input`; historical local reference), compiled FITS (`phase2/official/inputs/SNDATA_ROOT/kcor/DES/DES-SN5YR/calib_DES-SN5YR_DES.fits.gz`; historical local reference), and g/r/i/z DECam transmission files are locally present. The released nml names the uncompressed `.fits` basename, while the local copy is `.fits.gz`; source provenance of the executed fit, exact filter matching and filename resolution should be checked if an execution is attempted. The training submission refers to a historical `$DES_ROOT/SALT3training/calib_shiftlists_inclowZ` path, but the nine supplied `calib_shiftlists` copies match each model's embedded INFO records exactly. No missing DES nominal KCOR/filter asset is identified by this audit.

What has not been found among the released files is a row-matched, signed `dF_epoch/d(calibration or SALT mode)` for every accepted SN observation, with the direct zero-point/passband perturbation and the retrained-SALT response tied to the same correlated calibration draw. The existing exact-SNANA portable exports contain per-object nominal epoch means/covariances, and the [64-object local flux-response study](../../dust/notes/salt-dust-audit/flux-response.md) contains derivatives for its explicitly chosen dust/color templates; neither is the original release's shared calibration response. The distance covariance supplies no inverse map back to those epoch derivatives.

For a reproducible full-budget joint test, the concrete missing links are: the covariance and generation recipe for wavelength shifts and their cross-covariance with the available zero-point covariance, together with exact realization/seed mapping; the executed variation and nominal light-curve fit/BBC outputs (`MU`, `MUREF`, nuisance fits and CID/epoch selections) that formed the original covariance; and a documented common-mode likelihood or prior connecting the direct photometric shifts, retrained surface differences, and their amplitudes to the accepted measured fluxes. The released shift lists, upstream zero-point covariance and surface files provide a starting set of specific perturbations, but their existence alone does not supply that joint epoch likelihood or validate an arbitrary continuous extrapolation beyond them. Any new epoch-level fit would also have to handle shared calibration across all objects, retrain/refit where required, and propagate the changed selection/BBC response before claiming a corrected distance or cosmology result.

The current field-sign and held-out residual probabilities therefore quantify performance **conditional on the fixed global calibration and nominal trained surface used in those tests**. A coherent global mode may produce correlated signs across fields, so independent-field sign symmetry can fail under the release's systematic alternatives. Conversely, the existence of a distance-level `CALIBplusSALT3` covariance does not automatically explain the measured epoch pattern: testing that requires signed predictions in the actual epoch/filter/field design. Adding the distance systematic covariance to each per-object flux covariance, or adding it again after replacing it by explicit latent modes, would mix levels or double count uncertainty.
