This ledger concerns the acquired versions; a product is not raw luminosity merely because its column is called `m_b` or `MU`. Signs below are defined in the observed distance, not inferred from a parameter's name. Original inputs are preserved.

## Overlap and redshift conventions

STD-01 finds 1701 Pantheon+ measurements but only 1543 unique CIDs. Original DES has 1829 unique CIDs; Dovekie 1820. Across DES releases, 1718 objects match exactly by CID/survey: 1532 DES,114 Foundation,72 other low-z. Redshifts match exactly on this intersection, so the observed distance change is not a redshift-frame change. There are 111 original-only and102 Dovekie-only entries. This is more than the net nine-row size difference suggests.

Literal-ID matching to Pantheon+ identifies at least 335 shared original-DES CIDs and339 Dovekie CIDs; same-survey matches are332 and337. Shared observing calibration and SALT training create dependence even for different SNe. Aliases may increase overlap. No public cross-release distance covariance was used, so subtracting distances and adding their error bars in quadrature is not a valid significance test.

`zHD` is the CMB-frame redshift with peculiar-velocity correction; `zHEL` supplies the observed `(1+zHEL)` luminosity-distance factor, while the comoving integral uses zHD. The STD-03 low-z mass prescription explicitly uses **zCMB**, not zHD; boundary counts can change if substituted. Common absolute magnitude and H0 are degenerate in uncalibrated SN-only work.
