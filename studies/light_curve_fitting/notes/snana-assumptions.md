# Observation and measurement assumptions beneath the DES SNANA fit

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This bounded wave reached calibrated detector images as well as released light-curve fluxes. It found a substantial failure of a zero-baseline, unit-Gaussian noise model for the public **DIFFIMG** reduction. A baseline predicted from separate observing seasons removes most of its mean offset but leaves excess scatter, tails, and temporal structure. Those findings do **not** establish an SMP error or a cosmological bias. Matched SMP measurements have much smaller median offsets, and a three-exposure detector-plane check shows approximately unit local pixel-noise widths after source screening.

The machine-readable [assumption register](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/assumption_register.json) contains 13 claims, their exact pinned code/configuration sources, empirical falsifiers, results, and identifiability limits. The [initial preregistration](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/preregistration.json) predates the new residual/noise results. The cross-season baseline and detailed pixel tests have separately dated follow-up preregistrations, explicitly conditioned on what had already been learned. Original FITS and first-wave audit products remain unchanged. All new sources, scripts and outputs are SHA-256 inventoried in this wave's manifests.

## What SNANA actually assumes

The full likelihood is **not** an independent-epoch-error calculation. In the pinned current source, measurement variance enters the data covariance diagonal; optional `COVFUDGE_DATA` adds same-band covariance only when requested. The release config leaves that option blank. SALT model covariance and Milky Way extinction covariance are additional nonzero terms. Effective defaults are `OPT_COVAR_FLUX=1`, `OPT_COVAR_MWXTERR=1`, and `DOCHI2_SIGMA=F` / `OPT_CHI2_SIGMA=0`: the covariance is fixed within a later fit iteration and no determinant term is added by default. The [official agent's code register](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/official/fitter-assumptions.json) covers optimizer/model details. Relevant measurement code is [the covariance sum](https://github.com/RickKessler/SNANA/blob/886408a4e171896db5eaa97e735a655f50cec2db/src/snlc_fit.F90#L10694-L10712) and [optional data covariance](https://github.com/RickKessler/SNANA/blob/886408a4e171896db5eaa97e735a655f50cec2db/src/snlc_fit.F90#L14808-L14846).

The release uses signed flux, including negative observations. However, the default first-iteration SNR rejection is below −9, so “every negative flux is retained” would also be incorrect. Other first-iteration rejection uses the phase window and a diagonal no-fudge chi-square threshold of 10. The configured fit window is −15 to +45 rest-frame days; the initial peak-time and shape priors also matter. Published accepted residuals are therefore selected by the fit itself. These settings are in the [pinned DES fit configuration](https://github.com/des-science/DES-SN5YR/blob/e3493cb3b9505fc1f3f392d887364b50dae21439/7_PIPPIN_FILES/base_files/lcfit/lcfit_desSMP_5yr.nml#L18-L74).

Host-surface-brightness error scaling and DCR/chromatic corrections are already embedded in the released SMP fluxes/errors. The June 2024 configuration comments explicitly disable applying them a second time. The adopted Milky Way prescription is Schlafly-updated E(B−V), Fitzpatrick 1999 with Rv=3.1. Calibration FITS, bandpasses and SALT3.DES5YR are available, but using them successfully would demonstrate reproducibility, not independent correctness of their physical assumptions. Original and Dovekie PHOT tables are byte-identical; changes in their model/calibration/host products do not create an independent flux measurement.

## Repeated records and fit-mask conditioning

The first-wave full-column audit found 4,328 exact two-record repetitions across 1,550 DES objects. Most four-record groups with the same CID/exposure/MJD/band have an **A,B,A,B** pattern. A and B commonly differ in pixel coordinates, PSF, sky, gain or flags. Consequently, collapsing an entire exposure-key group to one record is not justified. The conservative unresolved concern is repeated information in exact full-column copies; the A/B measurement lineage remains uncertain. Published fits contain 374 accepted rows in 94 such key groups on 68 objects. This is evidence requiring a controlled information/covariance sensitivity test, not a quantified parameter or cosmological bias. See [exact repetitions](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/data_audit/exact_duplicates.json) and [detector-field comparisons](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/data_audit/duplicate_identity.json).

For the official agent's requested diagnostic, a **derived, flag-only** photometry set now retains the exact published accepted multiplicity: 62,702 rows across all 1,635 DES Hubble objects. It preserves all 1,798,736 original PHOT rows and every physical nonflag field; only dedicated rejection bit `536870912` is added to unaccepted records. All pre-existing flag bits are unchanged. There are 94 ambiguous matching groups; 93 retain all candidate multiplicities, while one has a nonunique subset assignment. Every assignment and ambiguity is recorded. This is conditional on the published mask and is not independent epoch selection. No deduplication occurred. Use [the conditional manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/conditional_mask/manifest.json), [assignments](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/conditional_mask/assignments.csv.gz), and [ambiguities](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/conditional_mask/ambiguity.json). The official agent owns execution and interpretation of the conditioned-mask fits.

## A SALT-independent off-signal flux check

The primary test used every original Hubble-sample DES CID with observations more than **365 observer days** from its published peak, comparing flux/error to a zero-transient-flux working model. It kept negative values. The DIFFIMG quality arm rejects its documented error bits and applies the released DES PSF and electron-zero-point cuts. That mask was not transplanted to SMP: published SMP fits accept some flag bits that DIFFIMG documents as errors. The sample is selected by eventual Hubble inclusion; it is not an unbiased sample of all survey detections.

| Public reduction / test | Rows | Objects | Mean pull | RMS pull | Robust MAD width | Fraction abs(pull)>3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DIFFIMG, >365d, quality | 444,195 | 1,635 | −0.861 | 2.944 | 1.403 | 12.62% |
| DIFFIMG, pre-peak <−365d | 227,694 | 1,262 | −0.417 | 1.964 | 1.188 | 6.25% |
| DIFFIMG, post-peak >365d | 216,501 | 1,353 | −1.327 | 3.705 | 1.729 | 19.33% |
| DIFFIMG, PIa>0.9, >365d | 385,795 | 1,418 | −0.894 | 2.770 | 1.397 | 12.50% |
| SMP, >200d secondary, quality | 26,109 | 832 | −0.0002 | 1.836 | 1.054 | 1.93% |

Here “pull” is signed FLUXCAL/FLUXCALERR and robust width is 1.4826 times median absolute deviation from the median. The unit-normal reference has a 0.27% two-sided >3 tail. The primary DIFFIMG mean has object-cluster-bootstrap 95% interval [−0.951,−0.776], RMS [2.656,3.296]. There are **no released SMP points beyond 365 days** for this sample: that primary test is unavailable for SMP, rather than passed. The >200-day SMP secondary test can include late transient flux and is not an equivalent zero-signal test. Its RMS is sensitive to a few objects: cluster interval [1.224,2.621]. Full band/field/host-surface-brightness tables are in [off-signal statistics](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/off_signal_statistics.json).

After seeing the first result, the specified follow-up trained a constant flux baseline for each SN×band on alternating **observing-season parities**, and predicted the other parity, then swapped folds. No point estimates its own baseline. The two error treatments add either formal weighted-baseline variance or the larger of that and an empirical night-cluster sandwich estimate. This is a diagnostic transformation only; it has not changed any analysis photometry.

| Same held-out support: 443,157 rows / 1,635 objects | Mean | RMS | MAD width | Fraction abs(pull)>3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original pulls | −0.845 | 2.904 | 1.400 | 12.48% |
| Predicted baseline removed; formal uncertainty | −0.017 | 1.913 | 1.238 | 6.15% |
| Predicted baseline removed; conservative night uncertainty | −0.038 | 1.664 | 1.225 | 5.16% |

The conservative RMS interval is [1.602,1.727]; the >3 tail interval is [4.71%,5.61%]. Thus a transferable constant offset explains much of the mean, but not all noise or variability. High-PIa objects still give RMS1.648 after that treatment. Bright host surfaces have greater residual scatter: RMS2.786 for host SB magnitude<22, 1.737 for22–24, and1.378 for≥24. These are observational associations, not proof that the host error model is the unique cause. Baseline uncertainty, reference-image contamination, remaining variability and changing observing conditions are not fully separated.

Neighboring held-out distinct-time residuals remain structured: for 1–10-day gaps, normalized uncentered products are approximately0.40/0.52/0.51/0.55 in g/r/i/z. These are **not** estimates of pure photon-noise correlation: common learned baselines and seasonal offsets can produce them. Nor can they be compared directly to simulation `REDCOV=0.6`. The simulator applies that coefficient to the **added error-fudge component**, in addition to separate search and template noise, not to total measured flux. Source convention is recorded in the assumption register and selection agent's report.

On 1,608 exact unique CID/IMGNUM/MJD/band matches at >200 days, spanning571 objects, the median SMP pull is−0.022 versus−0.572 for DIFFIMG, with median error ratio DIFFIMG/SMP1.001. These reductions share photons and have different processing/calibration; a quadrature-error significance for their difference would be unjustified. This comparison supports keeping the DIFFIMG finding scoped to its own reduction. See [follow-up results](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/off_signal_followup.json) and [matched records](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/cross_reduction_off_signal.csv.gz).

## The independent detector-plane check

The [DES access documentation](https://www.darkenergysurvey.org/the-des-project/data-access/) states that raw science and calibration images are public. The [Data Lab SIA documentation](https://datalab.noirlab.edu/docs/manual/UsingAstroDataLab/DataAccessInterfaces/SimpleImageAccessSIA/SimpleImageAccessSIA.html) exposes separate single-epoch and raw-image services. Availability was tested, not merely asserted: HTTP200 SIA queries for the first three original DES Hubble CIDs in numeric order returned10,10,228 rows. The initial CSV serialization could not represent vector-valued VOTable fields; [the successful lossless text-field parse](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/image_access/parsed_response_summary.json) preserves all rows. The first two positions yielded only Y-band exposures in this service; no different SN was substituted. CID1246314 had27 griz exposures with IDs matching released photometry.

The three nearest matched griz exposures were acquired for CID1246314, RA54.836567°, Dec−26.640186°, CCD17. Science FITS headers confirm individual exposures, dates, electron units, Gaia-DR2 WCS and seeing, despite the SIA response's misleading generic `proctype=Stack`. They are **calibrated single-exposure CCD images**, not raw unprocessed detector pixels and not coadds.

The weight endpoint ignored the small cutout size and returned the4096×2048 CCD. A0.4° science query still missed a small edge, so the1° query was retained separately and verified to cover the full CCD. Science cutouts were then matched **exactly, pixel for pixel** against integer crops of those full science frames, using their CRPIX differences; this established the corresponding weight crop. Weights are labeled `WGT`/`DES_EXT=WEIGHT`. Their median implied electron-noise values23.737,40.279,78.210 agree closely with header SKYSIGMA23.729,40.226,78.174. Complete-image URLs/hashes are in [the full-CCD manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/image_access/complete_science_manifest.json). Derived aligned stamps end in `.weight.aligned_stamp.fits`.

A fixed off-SN grid supplied60 unmasked control apertures in each stamp, with radius3 pixels and annulus8–12 pixels; the variance includes the Gaussian approximation to annulus-median uncertainty. All controls have RMS1.176,1.389,2.134, including astronomical sources. The separately reported source-screened subset and neighboring-pixel products≈0.004–0.011 give no gross local weight/noise failure in this bounded example. Screening on smoothed image signal can suppress tails, so these are **conditional diagnostics**, not a universal Gaussianity proof. The one-field, one-night sample and small aperture counts limit precision. [Pixel results](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/pixel_noise_results.json) and [all control apertures](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/pixel_control_apertures.csv) preserve both variants.

These DR2 processing products are not guaranteed to be the exact FinalCut inputs used in the SMP reprocessing. Exact per-object reference exposure selection, PSFEx models and their versions, tertiary-star calibration state, joint galaxy/position posterior and associated covariance have not been assembled in this wave. Their absence from the light-curve bundle is not evidence that no public counterpart exists anywhere. Full SMP extraction is therefore not reproduced, and the small aperture measurement is not compared to SMP amplitude without a matching zero point, host subtraction and PSF treatment. The primary [release paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05046) explains why SMP's joint galaxy model, reference choice and post-fit corrections make that distinction necessary.

## Published residuals and remaining inference boundary

All77,933 published data rows lie inside the published model-curve support. Linear interpolation of those2-day model samples gives accepted measurement-only RMS1.86/1.16/1.08/1.04 in g/r/i/z; signed square roots of the published diagonal total-error CHI2 have RMS0.974/0.936/0.911/0.904. Model uncertainty explains part of the difference. None is an independent SALT test: interpolation is approximate, the parameters were fit to these points, and clipping removed high residuals. The CHI2 column is not each point's unique contribution to the full covariance quadratic. [Per-row diagnostics](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/phase2/assumptions/published_residual_rows.csv.gz) preserve accepted and excluded observations separately.

The completed checks challenge measurement assumptions without replacing the photometry or assuming that SNANA is correct. They support testing exact-record information sensitivity and independent held-out-band/color physics once the baseline gate permits those fits. They do not establish that acceleration disappears, that a particular dust model wins, or that a measured DIFFIMG anomaly biases the published SMP cosmology. Those conclusions require a validated refit and selection-conditioned generative analysis.
