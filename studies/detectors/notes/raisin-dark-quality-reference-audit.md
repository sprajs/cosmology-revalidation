# Quality and reference audit of the 14 public dark-ramp candidates

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This metadata-only continuation preserves all six search-visit and eight template-visit candidates from the [RAW feasibility pilot](raisin-dark-raw-pilot.md). Its frozen primary-header phase reused the two local pilot RAW headers and fetched HTTP 206 byte ranges for the other twelve. It completed in 32.07 seconds and 241,920 network bytes, within the 90-second/2-MB cap. No SCI, ERR, DQ, SAMP, or TIME pixel arrays from the additional twelve were acquired or scored. The full per-root audit is in `runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/quality_reference_audit/candidate-quality-ledger.csv`.

All 14 headers are full-frame IR `SPARS50`, `NSAMP=16`, `EXPTIME=702.938171` s, with `CCDGAIN=2.5`. Eleven have `EXPFLAG=INDETERMINATE`; only `idbx43p7q`, `idbx41onq`, and `idp241psq` say `NORMAL`. `QUALITY`, `QUALCOM1`, and `QUALCOM2` are blank in every RAW primary header, which does not clear an exposure-time flag. `SAA_TIME` and `SAA_EXIT` vary and remain ledgered, without a derived quality cut. All candidates are retained, including the eleven flagged observations. The [MAST Archive Handbook](https://archive.stsci.edu/manuals/archive_handbook/appendix2.html) gives a generic, instrument-dependent meaning for `INDETERMINATE`: telemetry did not permit a successful exposure-time calculation and the predicted time was unavailable. The matching printed `SAMPTIME` schedule from the pilot does not prove telemetry completeness.

The listed MAST products for pilot template dark `idp247tnq` included its small SPT, TRL, and DADS LOG; no PDQ product was listed. All three were acquired under a separate frozen 90-second/10-MB phase (1,263,477 bytes). The [exact DADS log](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/quality_reference_audit/support_files/idp247tnq_log.txt) at lines 25–26 says TDF was down for **9 of 16 readouts** and explicitly sets `EXPFLAG` to `INDETERMINATE`. Lines 29–31 record an image-setup readout time of 2.91181 s and the 702.938171-s `EXPTIME`. The log does not identify which nine reads lacked TDF, so it cannot certify the first-eight telemetry. This direct explanation applies to `idp247tnq`; the other ten indeterminate candidates have not had support-log checks. It also does not establish detector noise quality or a reason to discard a root.

All 14 RAW headers name `iref$t2c16200i_ccd.fits` and `iref$q911321mi_osc.fits`. Their official CRDS HEAD sizes total 37,440 bytes, below the separately frozen 5-MB cap; exact files and hashes are preserved under `quality_reference_audit/reference_files/`. The locally pinned CALWF3 `getccdtab.c` matches CCDTAB on amplifier, nominal CCD gain, chip, offsets and binning, then copies per-amplifier A/D gain and read noise ([source lines 152–193](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/calwf3_variance_source/raw-processing-source/getccdtab.c#L152)). Among the four CCDTAB rows, row 2 is the unique observed `CCDAMP=ABCD`, `CCDGAIN=2.5` row. It lists A/B/C/D gain **2.34/2.37/2.31/2.38 electrons per DN** and read noise **20.2/19.8/19.9/20.1 electrons**. Those values match the two locally held science FLT primary headers after calibration. RAW `ATODGN*` and `READNSE*` zero values are unfilled placeholders, not zero physical gain or noise. The RAW primary header does not independently expose every source row-match field (chip/offset/bin), so uniqueness by the observed amplifier/gain and matching calibrated output is the execution-link evidence, not a proof from those two RAW keywords alone.

The OSCNTAB has one full-frame 1024×1024, ABCD row (row 1), with five-pixel trims on each edge and A/B reference-pixel spans 2–5 and 1020–1023; the other rows are smaller subarray shapes. The pinned CALWF3 `doir.c` loads overscan information through `FindOverscan` after CCDTAB ([lines 151–160](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_hst_pixel_feasibility/calwf3_variance_source/source-3.7.3/doir.c#L151)). These are reference definitions for a future prefix processing test, not a measurement of reference-pixel noise. The four RAW pilot files remain untouched; no remaining dark image acquisition or covariance analysis was run.

`quality_reference_audit/result.json` gives counts, selected table rows, units and the explicit support-log evidence. `manifest.json` freezes the network protocols, original primary-header hashes, support products, reference tables and source pointers. The varied BPIXTAB and CRDS context values for the two visits are preserved per object in the CSV; no global-quality inference is made from their names.
