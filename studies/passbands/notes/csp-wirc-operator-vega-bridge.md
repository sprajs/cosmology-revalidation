# CSP WIRC-J reference bridge: pinned SNooPy VegaB convention

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This is an additive audit to [physical WIRC-J feasibility](csp-wirc-operator-feasibility.md). It preserves the earlier published-CSP-count-zero-point calculation and does not generate KCOR, fit an object, choose a source version by agreement, or adjust a magnitude. The [comparison protocol](../specifications/experiments/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/comparison-protocol.json) (SHA-256 `4648b0ded4c4a89f91a13aa0e4bc397d47fccaccf1f26b054f6d19289e5b5acb`) was frozen before the seven magnitudes were computed.

The inspected SNooPy registry is `obscode/snpy` commit `3b97ef08e48b4d535f587d19a14eb2d0ea3a7f33`; every acquired byte was checked against its Git blob ([source ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/acquisition.json), [curve ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/curve-acquisition.json)). Its [Vega standards registry](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/snpy/snpy/filters/standards/Vega/standards.dat#L1) binds **`VegaB` to `alpha_lyr_stis_005.ascii`**, not to the similarly named `Vega_Bohlin.dat` that an initial source-only acquisition had included. The latter was preserved but not used; the source-derived [amendment](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/acquisition-amendment.json) identifies the operative file. All six Swope/du Pont filter declarations plus WIRC `Jdw` say `VegaB=0`; all seven acquired curves are byte-identical to the pinned CSP scan files. `VegaB=0` tells [SNooPy `compute_zpt`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/snpy/snpy/filters/__init__.py#L161) to set the Vega synthetic magnitude to zero. Its [`response`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/snpy/snpy/filters/__init__.py#L211) uses photon weighting λ/(hc), with pinned module defaults linear filter interpolation and Simpson integration (lines 28–35, 290–307); [registry parsing](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/snpy/snpy/filters/__init__.py#L713) applies that zero point.

The comparison uses the **RAISIN KCOR's explicitly declared** `bd_17d4708_stisnic_007.dat`, not an alternate SNooPy `bd17` alias. Indeed the pinned [SNooPy Smith registry](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/snpy/snpy/filters/standards/Smith/standards.dat#L1) names `bd17` as a Fukugita-table spectrum and `bd17B` as STIS/NIC **006**; neither is RAISIN's declared **007**. That source-version difference was recorded after the outcome and **no alternate BD17 spectrum was calculated**. The seven fixed mappings and source hashes are in the protocol.

The [frozen comparison result](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/comparison-result.json) computes `m_BD17 = −2.5 log10(F_BD17/F_VegaB)` with the pinned SNooPy-default Simpson prescription. An independent union-grid trapezoid was a prespecified numerical check. Their differences reached **3.873 millimag** for RC1 J, larger than the input's printed precision. A separately declared, **post-result numerical diagnostic** ([protocol](../specifications/experiments/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/quadrature-diagnostic-protocol.json), [result](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/quadrature-diagnostic-result.json)) exactly integrates the cubic product of the same piecewise-linear filter and star SED on each union-knot interval. That exact piecewise result agrees with union-grid trapezoid within 1.2×10⁻⁶ mag. It diagnoses quadrature; it does not replace the frozen default-SNooPy comparison or select a magnitude based on release agreement.

The preregistered printed-half-digit test passes **0/6** for default SNooPy integration; the post-result exact piecewise diagnostic passes **1/6** (`h`). Exact-piecewise discrepancies in the six declared RAISIN bands are all under 0.55 millimag, but they are not identically zero and their signs differ. VegaB removes most of the earlier RC2 gap from the CSP README branch (which gave RC2 8.420644 versus released 8.4192), yet neither the source-default SNooPy calculation nor the integration-stable VegaB result reproduces all six numbers at their printed precision. In particular, WIRC J is **8.420283** under the exact-piecewise VegaB convention versus **8.422291** under the earlier CSP README count zero point. These are *conditional reference calculations*, not competing calibrated KCOR outputs from which to pick whichever yields a desired SN response. Even their WIRC−RC2 color differences are not the same.

The inspected author RAISIN KCOR input (`sources/repos/djones1040__RAISIN_DataRelease@a383c4b/kcor/kcor_CSPDR3_BD17.input:20`; historical local reference) contains fixed `FILTER` MAGREF literals, not a `VegaB` specification. The pinned author [`raisin_cosmo/kcor.py`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/author/raisin_cosmo/kcor.py) investigates SN SED K-corrections but contains no call to SNooPy `synth_mag`, `synthetic_magnitude` or a KCOR generator; it supplies no execution-linked derivation of those six literals ([author blob identity](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_wirc_operator_feasibility/vega_bridge/author-acquisition.json)). This is an absence of a link in inspected assets, not proof of how the historical author chose the numbers. An exact physical WIRC branch would require that provenance or a declared new, internally consistent primary-reference convention; no offset or source-version switch was inserted here.
