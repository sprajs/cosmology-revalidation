# CSP native covariance amplitude check

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This independent, read-only check uses all 42 objects from the existing full-cohort nominal native log. Its [protocol](../specifications/experiments/csp_covariance_independent/protocol.json) and [executable freeze](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/execution-freeze.json) precede numeric score inspection. No native fit, altered input, object exclusion, or alternate distance optimization was performed. The [per-object ledger](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/per-object.csv) preserves every term.

The pinned `snlc_fit.car` source constructs flux covariance as model-flux covariance, MW-extinction covariance, and data covariance (lines 10172–10223). The data diagonal is `JEP_DATAFLUX_ERR² + JEP_FUDGEFLUX_ERR²`, with optional `COVFUDGE_DATA`. For this executed NML, `FUDGE_MAG_COVERR` is blank, `FUDGE_DATAERR_SCALE=1`, `FUDGE_FITERR_MAXFRAC=0`, and `FUDGEALL_ITER1_MAXFRAC=.02` acts only on the first iteration (`GET_FUDGE_FITERR_MAXFRAC`, lines 10656–10700). Thus for iterations 2 and 3, `E=diag(exported data error²)`. Both model error and MW-extinction flux error scale with the model amplitude (`snlc_fit.car` lines 10172–10210, 10500–10521). With fixed rows, shape, AV, and peak, write `C(D)=E+a(D)²M`. The source returns the inverse covariance, so this check first inverts exported `W₂` and `W₃`.

All 42 final row/data sets match their preceding iteration exactly. Predicting `C₃ = E + 10^−0.8(D₂−D₁) (`docs/research-2026-09-26/C₂−E`; historical local reference)` reproduces the exported `C₃`: worst relative Frobenius difference **8.01×10⁻⁸** (CID `2004eo`); worst error in data-error-whitened entries **1.03×10⁻⁵**. The final model mean itself follows `f₃/f₂=10^[−0.4(D₃−D₂)]` to at most 1.38×10⁻¹⁰. The minimum eigenvalue of the data-error-whitened `C₂−E` across objects is **0.00539**; no PSD adjustment was made. The small covariance residual is consistent with native stored/printed precision, rather than an exact symbolic identity claim.

At each final iteration-3 state, the covariance was built at **D₂**, while the model mean and residual are evaluated at fitted **D₃**. With `k=0.4 ln(10)`, `f_D=−kf₃`, `C_D=−2k(C₃−E)`, `W=W₃`, and `r=y−f₃`, the [result](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/result.json) reports the three terms of the local derivative of Gaussian `−2 log L`: `−2 f_DᵀWr`, `−rᵀWC_DWr`, and `tr(WC_D)`. Across the 42 objects their separate ranges, in per-mag units, are −0.11521 to +0.0000582, +9.915 to +5421.034, and −123.728 to −4.235; total score ranges +4.042 to +5317.466. The large nonzero covariance terms show that a small *fixed-C mean* gradient is not the same as stationarity of a normalized, parameter-dependent-C likelihood. The native `FITCHI2` optimizer does not include this log-determinant score term (`snlc_fit.car` around lines 4832–4852); these numbers are an algebra diagnostic, not a replacement native objective or evidence of physical noise misspecification.

One auxiliary model-mean-ratio field in the first execution compared the wrong pair of distance iterations. The [correction](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/mean-ratio-correction.json) was frozen before recomputation; original source/result bytes remain in [correction history](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/tree/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/correction-history). Covariance predictions, eigenvalues, and all score terms were unaffected. The final script and output hashes are in the [manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_covariance_independent/manifest.json).
