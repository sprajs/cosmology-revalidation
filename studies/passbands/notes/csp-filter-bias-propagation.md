# CSP J-filter intervention and existing RAISIN bias correction

> Supporting research note. Scientific findings and limitations are preserved from the recorded investigation; historical result links identify the evidence available at that time. See the [study guide](../../README.md) for current execution status.

This is a source-only map for propagating the frozen `Jdw→j` label intervention. It does not run or select simulation outcomes, refit light curves, change a distance, or recalculate a bias correction. The [asset manifest](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/acquisition.json) pins 12 author-repository Git blobs at commit `b888214a5cbae38ac0bf886488ce7734f5b77e87`; the [SIMLIB label audit](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/simlib-label-audit.json) is metadata-only.

The intended nominal CSP generator input, [`SIMGEN_CSP_SNOOPY.input`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/author/sim/inputs/lowz/SIMGEN_CSP_SNOOPY.input#L15), uses `CSPDR3.SIMLIB`, the SNooPy B18 grid, `OIR.J19` correlated scatter, and `kcor_CSPDR3_BD17.fits` (lines 15, 35–44). It allows `GENFILTERS=ugriBnmoJjYyH`, copies the SIMLIB peak date, smears the search peak by 0.01 day, and applies search and generation cuts (lines 26–29, 47–75). Its latent population has stretch peak 0.913, asymmetric sigmas 0.194/0.201 over 0.7–1.3; `A_V` exponential scale 0.2 mag over 0–2; and `R_V` peak 1.518 with 0.001/0.001 generator sigmas (lines 92–99). The `STSYS` file instead uses stretch peak 1.017 and sigmas 0.271/0.278; both `AVSYS` files use `A_V` scale 0.15. These are source settings, not a measured latent distribution or a hash-linked assertion that these exact bytes generated the archived simulation FITRES. The [master input](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/author/sim/inputs/lowz/SIMGEN_MASTER_LOWZ.INPUT) lists those CSP versions and a nominal `OIR.J19` override, but has different per-version `NGENTOT_LC` text than the individual input. Effective generation command, parameter precedence, seeds and completed photometry identity are not supplied by the acquired files.

The [nominal SIMLIB](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/author/sim/simlibs/CSPDR3.SIMLIB#L7) declares **30 cadence libraries and 5,374 epoch rows**, of which **428 are `J` and zero are `j`**. Its own header says it was made from `CSPDR3_RAISIN` photometry, and its rows carry only band tokens, not original instrument identifiers. Of the frozen 42-object cohort's 188 independently identified WIRC-`Jdw` rows in 32 objects, **146 rows in 21 objects** match this SIMLIB by source CID comment, MJD at its printed 0.001-day precision, and `J` band; eleven affected cohort CIDs have no matching cadence library. Thus the available nominal generator can synthesize those copied WIRC epochs only under the existing `J` operator. The fact that `j` is permitted by `GENFILTERS` does not make any `j` observations appear in the nominal SIMLIB. The alternative `LOWZ_CSP_MODFILT.SIMLIB` is commented out in the nominal generator input and is a separate asset, not evidence of the nominal execution.

The released KCOR definition (`sources/repos/djones1040__RAISIN_DataRelease@a383c4b/kcor/kcor_CSPDR3_BD17.input:20`; historical local reference) maps `J` to RetroCam RC1 and `j` to RC2; it does not contain a physical WIRC-J passband. The original CSP photometry chain relabels WIRC `Jdw` as `J`, preserving its magnitude and error at that link (see [passband lineage](csp-passband-lineage.md)); the earlier 42-object identification is therefore a physical-instrument ledger, not a property recoverable from the SIMLIB alone. The [CSP DR3 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05146) groups WIRC J with RC2 on stellar color-calibration grounds, but that approximation does not make arbitrary SN spectra identical in WIRC and RC2. A `Jdw→j` native response measures a processing-label change under the released empirical model and KCOR table, not a full WIRC throughput reconstruction.

The [simulation NIR fit configuration](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/author/fit/sim/REFAC_CSP_RAISIN_NIR.nml#L37) uses the same KCOR/grid, fits `YyJjH`, and fixes stretch at 1, `A_V` at 0, `R_V` at 1.518 and peak time (lines 70–88). The [NIR SUBMIT.INFO](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/csp_filter_bias_propagation/author/output/fit_nir/CSP_RAISIN_NIR_SIM/SUBMIT.INFO) names that NML and four nominal/shape/extinction versions, reporting 12 completed jobs on 2021-11-11. The optical+NIR companion fitter instead permits stretch, AV and peak to vary. This matches the [RAISIN paper's](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07801v2#S3.SS2) explanation: NIR distance is an amplitude-only fit, while optical data supply peak and selection information; population-dependent average shape/extinction effects are handled by simulation bias correction. The paper says the SNooPy templates and optical-to-NIR dispersion were built using low-z CSP data and the simulation models correlated interband scatter, cadences, selection, shape and extinction ([Sections III.1–III.5 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07801v2#S11)).

The pinned author [`cosmo_sys.py`](https://github.com/sprajs/cosmology-revalidation/blob/17487bf659fcbdeeea072221492bac14b04a0a85/runs/research_2026_09_26/raisin_differential/mass-threshold/code/raisin_cosmo/cosmo_sys.py#L339) selects the nominal or `STSYS`/`AVSYS` simulation fit products according to FITOPT tags, applies shape/AV cuts from optical+NIR fits to real and simulated objects (lines 310–335, 339–395), then estimates each CSP distance bias from nearby-redshift simulated `DLMAG−SIM_DLMAG` with inverse-variance weights and subtracts it from the real NIR distance (lines 400–413). This code is a path and algebra witness, not a proved invocation for every released distance row. The paper likewise reports that NIR bias corrections are dominated by differences in shape and extinction populations and includes shape/AV variations in its systematic budget ([III.5, III.7.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07801v2#S3.SS5)). Its CSP J/H calibration systematic shifts are common magnitude offsets, not an explicit `Jdw→RC2` identity intervention.

For a matched label-response comparison, the simulation **generation**, NIR and optical+NIR refits, accepted masks/selection, and bias-estimation step would have to use a single declared nominal or changed observation operator. The known WIRC cadence rows would need the same independently identified relabel as the real rows; `j` would then use the existing RC2 KCOR approximation in both simulation and fit. Recompute a changed-operator bias from its own fitted and selected simulations, and replace the nominal bias when forming a changed-operator distance. Adding a changed raw-distance response to an already corrected released distance *and also retaining or reapplying an unrelated nominal bias* would silently mix operators or double count a correction. A paired seed/latent/cadence design could isolate the operator response, but its execution, acceptance and full selection closure must be shown rather than assumed.

Even that matched `Jdw→j` simulation cannot repair the empirical SNooPy B18 templates or interband dispersion learned from overlapping CSP data under their original filter convention. The released model grid has no independent WIRC response, and the paper explicitly says its NIR templates and scatter estimates use CSP. Re-training or independently validating the template under a corrected physical passband, with calibration uncertainty, is separate work. Missing for full release closure are generator completion logs and hashes tying this input/SIMLIB/KCOR/model/scatter to archived simulated photometry, full generated-attempt and selection ledgers, run-time expanded NML/executable and original training row/filter provenance. No assumption about those missing links was substituted here.
