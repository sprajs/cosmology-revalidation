First select 12 objects by equally spaced redshift ranks among 1635 official DES SNe; then expand to all official DES objects if runtime permits. Selection cannot depend on fitted residuals. Audit any mismatch with SNANA's iterative rejection, priors, model covariance and calibration conventions explicitly.
5. Validate by per-object parameter differences, normalized differences, flux residuals by phase/filter and failure accounting. Do not claim the independent fitter is numerically identical to SNANA. Do not use discrepant fits for cosmology before identifying their cause.
6. Trace BBC4D correction and contamination inputs to exact public assets; state which parts have actually been regenerated. Freeze any subsequent changes as amendments motivated by implementation evidence. No alternative correction-model comparison until this baseline boundary is explicit.

Interpretation rule: outcomes may support or oppose any physical correction. Agreement with a published corrected Hubble diagram is necessary implementation evidence, not proof of the underlying model. Raw observables and full covariance are required for later comparisons.
